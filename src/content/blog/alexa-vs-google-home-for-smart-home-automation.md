## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the difference between Alexa and Google Home?
**Alexa** and **Google Home** are both voice assistants, but they have different features and capabilities. **Alexa** is compatible with over **10,000 smart home devices**, while **Google Home** offers **multi-room audio** capabilities. 
### How do I set up Alexa?
To set up **Alexa**, simply download the **Amazon Alexa app** and follow the instructions. You will need a **stable Wi-Fi connection** and an **Amazon account**. 
### Can I use Alexa with Google Home devices?
No, **Alexa** and **Google Home** are not compatible with each other. However, you can use **Alexa** with **Google Nest** devices, such as the **Google Nest Thermostat**. 
### What is the range of Alexa?
The range of **Alexa** is approximately **30 feet**, depending on the device and the environment. 
### Can I use Google Home with Amazon devices?
No, **Google Home** is not compatible with **Amazon devices**, such as the **Amazon Fire TV**. 
### How do I reset Alexa?
To reset **Alexa**, simply press and hold the **microphone button** and the **volume down button** at the same time for **10 seconds**.
