## Designate a 'Touchdown Zone' for Paperwork and Mail
The 'touchdown zone' concept, introduced by Marie Kondo in [The Life-Changing Magic of Tidying Up](https://www.amazon.com/Life-Changing-Magic-Tidying-Up), involves designating a specific area for paperwork and mail. This can help keep important documents tidy and make it easier to find what you need. Here are the steps to create a 'touchdown zone':
1. Choose a specific area for your 'touchdown zone'
2. Use a file organizer to keep important documents tidy
3. Set up a system for handling paperwork and mail

## Implement a 'One In, One Out' Policy for Office Supplies
The 'one in, one out' policy, introduced by Chris Bailey in [The Productivity Project](https://www.amazon.com/The-Productivity-Project), involves getting rid of an old item every time you bring a new one into your workspace. This can help maintain a balanced supply of office materials. Here are some examples of apps and tools that can help track inventory:
* [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets) for tracking inventory
