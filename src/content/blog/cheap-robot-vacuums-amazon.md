## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the cheapest reliable robot vacuum?
The **Eufy RoboVac 11** at $159 is the most reliable budget option, with **4.3/5 stars** from 12,000+ Amazon reviews. It handles pet hair and hard floors effectively.

### Do cheap robot vacuums work on carpets?
Yes, but performance varies. iRobot 694 removes **89% of debris** from medium-pile carpets (Wirecutter, 2023), while basic models like Eufy manage **75-80%**.

### How often should I replace robot vacuum filters?
Every **3 months** for optimal performance. A clogged filter reduces suction by **up to 40%** (CNET, 2023). Washable filters last 6-12 months with weekly cleaning.

### Can budget robot vacuums avoid stairs?
All modern models have **cliff sensors**, preventing falls. In tests, they detect drops with **99.7% accuracy** from 1+ inch heights (IEEE Robotics, 2022).

### What's the lifespan of affordable robot vacuums?
**2-4 years** with proper maintenance. The motor typically fails first, lasting **8,000-10,000 hours** in budget models vs. 15,000+ in premium ones (Consumer Reports, 2023).
