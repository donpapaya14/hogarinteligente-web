## Setting Up Voice Control with Your Smart Lighting
To set up voice control with your smart lighting, follow these steps:
1. Choose a smart home assistant, such as **Alexa** or **Google Home**.
2. Download the smart home assistant app and create an account.
3. Connect your smart lighting to the app.
4. Use voice commands to control your smart lighting.
For more information, visit [Amazon Alexa Developer Tools](https://developer.amazon.com).

## Customizing Your Smart Lighting for Energy Efficiency
To customize your smart lighting for energy efficiency, follow these tips:
* Schedule your lights to turn off when not in use.
* Use geofencing to turn off lights when you leave the house.
* Adjust the brightness and color of your lights to reduce energy consumption.
According to [Energy Star](https://www.energystar.gov), smart home energy efficiency tips can help you save up to **$100** per year.

## Security and Safety Considerations for Smart Lighting
When it comes to smart lighting, security and safety are important considerations. Make sure to:
* Use **data encryption** to protect your smart lighting data.
* Set up **emergency shutdown** procedures in case of a power outage.
For more information, visit [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov).

## Troubleshooting Common Smart Lighting Issues
Here are some common smart lighting issues and their solutions:
* Connectivity problems: Check your internet connection and restart your smart lighting device.
* Compatibility issues: Check the compatibility of your smart lighting device with your smart home assistant.
