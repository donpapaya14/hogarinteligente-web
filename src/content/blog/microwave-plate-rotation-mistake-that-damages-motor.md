## Introduction to Microwave Plate Rotation Mistake
The **microwave plate rotation mistake** can lead to premature motor failure, resulting in costly repairs or even replacement of the entire appliance. According to the Appliance Repair Technical Bulletin (2022), uneven weight distribution, such as using oversized dishes, can strain the motor, leading to **torque resistance** of up to 30% more than the recommended limit.

## Why the Turntable Motor Fails Prematurely
The turntable motor in a microwave is designed to rotate the plate at a consistent speed, ensuring even cooking. However, when the weight distribution is uneven, the motor is forced to work harder, leading to increased wear and tear. A study by the Appliance Repair Technical Bulletin (2022) found that **75% of microwave motor failures** are caused by uneven weight distribution.

## The 30-Second Balancing Test
To check the load balance of your microwave turntable, follow these steps:
1. Place a coin on the turntable.
2. Rotate the turntable by hand.
3. If the coin stays in place, the turntable is balanced. If it falls off, the turntable is unbalanced.
