## Why Sticky Garlic Ruins Most Gadgets
Sticky garlic can ruin most gadgets due to its high viscosity, which is caused by the allicin content. A study published in the Journal of Food Science (2018) found that allicin has a viscosity of **1.4 Pa·s**, making it one of the most viscous compounds found in food. This viscosity causes garlic to stick to surfaces, making it difficult to clean and maintain gadgets.

## Pro Trick to Prevent Sticking
Even with the best garlic press, sticking can still occur. A pro trick to prevent sticking is to rub the press with **olive oil** before use. According to America's Test Kitchen (2022), this reduces adhesion by **60%**. Simply rub a small amount of olive oil onto the press and wipe off any excess before use.

## Why Most 'Dishwasher Safe' Claims are Misleading
Many garlic presses claim to be dishwasher safe, but this can be misleading. According to a study published in the Materials Engineering Journal (2021), detergent enzymes can degrade rubber gaskets over time, causing them to become brittle and prone to cracking. This can lead to a buildup of bacteria and other contaminants, making the press less safe to use.
