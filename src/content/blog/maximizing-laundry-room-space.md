## Introduction to Laundry Room Organization
Maximizing **laundry room organization** is crucial for a seamless and efficient laundry experience. According to the **National Association of Professional Organizers (2022)**, a well-organized laundry room can save up to 30 minutes per day. Common challenges include cluttered spaces and inadequate storage, which can be addressed with a **minimalist laundry room** approach.

## Purging and Categorizing Laundry Supplies
To start, purge and categorize laundry supplies, including detergent, fabric softener, and stain removers. Follow this step-by-step guide:
1. Sort supplies into categories.
2. Discard expired or unused items.
3. Store items in labeled containers.
As **The Spruce (2023)** suggests, consider using a caddy or cart to keep supplies organized and within reach.

## Utilizing Vertical Space with Shelves
Installing shelves and using wall-mounted storage can maximize vertical space in a laundry room. **This Old House (2022)** recommends:
1. Measure the space to determine the optimal shelf size.
2. Choose a sturdy material, such as wood or metal.
3. Install shelves at a comfortable height to reduce strain.

## Optimizing Laundry Room Lighting
Improving lighting in the laundry room can enhance visibility and ambiance. **Lighting Design Lab (2022)** suggests:
1. Use a combination of overhead and task lighting.
2. Choose LED bulbs for energy efficiency and longevity.
3. Consider installing a skylight or larger window to increase natural light.

## Implementing a 'One-Touch' Laundry System
A 'one-touch' laundry system can reduce laundry time and increase efficiency. **FlyLady (2023)** recommends:
1. Sort clothes as soon as they are removed.
2. Pre-treat stains immediately.
3. Fold and put away clothes as soon as the cycle is complete.
