## Introduction to Pet Hair Challenges
The **American Pet Products Association (APPA)** 2022 reports that approximately 67% of households in the United States own a pet, which translates to around 70 million households. Pet owners face the challenge of dealing with **pet hair shedding**, with some breeds shedding more than others. According to the APPA, the average dog sheds around **1-2 pounds of hair per year**. This can lead to common cleaning problems such as clogged vacuums and allergies.

## Key Features for Pet Hair Removal
When it comes to removing pet hair, **suction power**, **filtration systems**, and **brush types** are crucial. **Consumer Reports 2023** recommends looking for a vacuum with a suction power of at least **20 air watts**. Additionally, a **HEPA filter** can help trap 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns, including pet dander and allergens.

## Maintenance and Cleaning Tips
To maintain your robot vacuum's performance, it's essential to **clean the brushes and filters regularly**. According to **iRobot Customer Support 2022**, you should clean the brushes every 1-2 weeks and replace the filters every 2-3 months. Here are some practical tips:
1. Check and clean the brushes after each use.
2. Replace the filters every 2-3 months.
3. Empty the dustbin after each use.
