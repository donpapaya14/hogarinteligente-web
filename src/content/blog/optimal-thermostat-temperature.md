## Introduction to Energy Saving

Setting your thermostat to the right **energy saving temperature** can reduce household energy consumption by **10-15% annually**, according to the [U.S. Department of Energy (2022)](https://www.energy.gov). Heating and cooling account for nearly **50% of home energy use**, making thermostat settings a critical factor in efficiency. The key is balancing comfort with cost savings by optimizing temperature ranges seasonally.

## Ideal Winter Temperature

During winter, the **optimal thermostat setting** is **68-70°F (20-21°C)** when awake and **60-62°F (15-16°C)** when sleeping or away. The [National Resources Defense Council (2020)](https://www.nrdc.org) found this range reduces heating costs by **5-10% per degree lowered** over 8 hours. For every 1°F decrease, you save **1% on your heating bill**.

**Winter efficiency tips:**
1. Use programmable thermostats to auto-adjust temperatures overnight.
2. Layer clothing or use blankets to compensate for lower settings.
3. Seal windows and doors to prevent heat loss (drafts can increase energy use by **20%**).

## Ideal Summer Temperature

In summer, set thermostats to **78-80°F (25-27°C)** when home and **85°F (29°C)** when away. The [American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy (2019)](https://www.aceee.org) reports this can cut cooling costs by **6-8% per degree raised**. Ceiling fans allow you to raise the thermostat by **4°F without sacrificing comfort**.

Set it to **85°F** instead of turning it off. Cooling a hot house requires more energy than maintaining a higher baseline (DOE, 2022).
