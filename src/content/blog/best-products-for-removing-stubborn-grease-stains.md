According to [The American Cleaning Institute](https://www.cleaninginstitute.org), grease stains are composed of triglycerides and waxes, which adhere to surfaces due to their hydrophobic nature.

## Pre-Treatment Techniques for Grease Stains
Pre-treating grease stains can improve the effectiveness of stain removers. Here are some tips and tricks:
1. Apply **baking soda** to the stain and let it sit for 30 minutes.
2. Use **white vinegar** to dissolve the grease.
3. Apply **hydrogen peroxide** to the stain and let it sit for 10-15 minutes.

## DIY Grease Stain Removers and Pre-Treatment Methods
