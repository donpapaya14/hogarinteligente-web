## Introduction to Spiralizers
The **best spiralizer for zucchini noodles** is a kitchen gadget that can help you create healthy and delicious meals. According to [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com), a spiralizer is a tool that uses a rotating blade to cut vegetables into noodle-like strands. 

## What is a Spiralizer and How Does it Work?
A spiralizer works by using a **rotating blade** to cut vegetables into strands. There are different types of spiralizers, including manual and electric models. Manual spiralizers are **less expensive** and easier to clean, while electric spiralizers are **faster** and more efficient.
