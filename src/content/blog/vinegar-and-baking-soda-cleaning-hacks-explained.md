## Introduction to Vinegar and Baking Soda Cleaning
The combination of **vinegar and baking soda** is a powerful tool for natural cleaning, thanks to the acid-base reaction between acetic acid and sodium bicarbonate, which creates effervescence, lifting dirt and neutralizing odors. According to the [American Chemical Society](https://www.acs.org), this reaction is effective but not suitable for all surfaces due to pH levels.

## Why Vinegar and Baking Soda Work Together
The acid-base reaction is the key to the effectiveness of **vinegar and baking soda cleaning**. The reaction between acetic acid (a weak organic acid) and sodium bicarbonate (a weak base) produces carbon dioxide gas, water, and salt. This reaction is exothermic, meaning it releases heat, which helps to lift dirt and grime. A study by the [American Chemical Society](https://www.acs.org) in 2018 found that this reaction is effective in removing dirt and grime from surfaces.

## Exact Ratios for Maximum Cleaning Power
To achieve maximum cleaning power, it's essential to use the right ratios of **vinegar and baking soda**. For example, a 1:1 ratio is effective for cleaning drains, while a 2:1 paste is better for grout. However, using too much vinegar can damage delicate surfaces. The [University of Nebraska-Lincoln Extension](https://extension.unl.edu) recommends using a 1:1 ratio for most cleaning tasks.

## Unexpected Surfaces to Avoid
While **vinegar and baking soda** are effective cleaning agents, there are some surfaces that should be avoided. These include stone countertops, hardwood floors, and aluminum, as **vinegar** can etch these surfaces and **baking soda** can cause abrasion. The [National Association of Home Builders](https://www.nahb.org) recommends using alternative cleaning methods for these surfaces.

## Pro Trick: Add Salt for Scrubbing Power
Adding **coarse salt** to the **vinegar and baking soda** mixture can transform it into a non-toxic scrub for tough cleaning tasks, such as cleaning oven racks or pots. The [Cleaning Science Review](https://www.cleaningsciencereview.com) recommends using kosher salt or sea salt for this purpose.

## The 10-Minute Rule for Drain Cleaning
To clean drains effectively, follow the 10-minute rule:
1. Pour **baking soda** down the drain
2. Wait 2 minutes
3. Add **vinegar** to the drain
4. Let it bubble for 5 minutes
5. Flush the drain with boiling water
This method prevents pipe damage and is recommended by the [Plumbing Manufacturers International](https://www.pmihome.org).

## Boost with Citrus for Lingering Freshness
Soaking **orange peels** in **vinegar** for 2 weeks can create a citrus-infused cleaner that masks odors and cuts grease. The [Journal of Environmental Health](https://www.jstor.org/journal/jenvheal) recommends storing this mixture in a [Mason jar with measurement markings](AMAZON:Mason Jar) and using it within 6 months.
