## Measuring and Choosing the Right Size
When choosing an over-the-door storage rack, it's essential to measure your door to ensure the right fit. According to [The Spruce](https://www.thespruce.com), you should measure the width of your door and choose a rack that is **1-2 inches** narrower. You should also consider the weight capacity of the rack and choose one that can hold the items you plan to store.

## Installation and Maintenance Tips
Installing an over-the-door storage rack is relatively easy, but it's essential to follow the manufacturer's instructions. According to [Bob Vila](https://www.bobvila.com), you should start by attaching the hooks to the door and then hang the rack. To maintain your rack, you should clean it regularly with a damp cloth and avoid overloading it.
