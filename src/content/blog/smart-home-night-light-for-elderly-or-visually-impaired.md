## Tips for Installing and Using a Smart Night Light
To install and use a smart night light, follow these steps:
1. **Choose a location** with a power outlet and minimal obstruction.
2. **Adjust the brightness** to a comfortable level.
3. **Configure motion sensing** to turn on the light when you enter the room.
4. **Schedule the light** to turn on and off at specific times.
