## Understanding the Difference Between Alexa and Google Home
The main difference between **Alexa** and **Google Home** lies in their respective ecosystems and voice assistant capabilities. According to [Amazon (2023)](https://www.amazon.com), Alexa boasts a wider range of compatible devices, with over **100,000** smart home products available. In contrast, [Google (2023)](https://www.google.com) reports that Google Home supports over **10,000** devices.

## Voice Assistant Capabilities and Accuracy
In terms of voice recognition accuracy, **Google Home** outperforms Alexa, with a **92%** accuracy rate compared to Alexa's **88%**, according to a study by [Northwestern University (2020)](https://www.northwestern.edu). Additionally, Google Home's natural language understanding capabilities are more advanced, allowing for more complex commands.
