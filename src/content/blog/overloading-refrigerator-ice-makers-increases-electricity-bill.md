## The Shocking Truth: How Overloading Refrigerator Ice Makers Affects Energy Consumption
Overloading your refrigerator's ice maker can increase **energy consumption by up to 20%** due to the increased compressor usage, according to the [U.S. Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov/) (2020). This is a significant impact on your electricity bill, especially considering the average household uses their ice maker frequently.

## The Impact of Ice Maker Overloading on Your Electricity Bill
A study by the [National Resources Defense Council](https://www.nrdc.org/) (2019) found that the average household spends around **$100 per year** on excess energy consumption due to overloading appliances like the ice maker. This can be avoided by maintaining your refrigerator's ice maker and ensuring it's not overloaded.

## How to Check if Your Ice Maker is Overloaded
To check if your ice maker is overloaded, follow these steps:
1. Check your ice maker's manual for the recommended ice production capacity.
2. Measure the amount of ice produced by your ice maker in a 24-hour period.
3. Compare the measured amount to the recommended capacity.

## Tips to Reduce Energy Consumption from Your Refrigerator's Ice Maker
To reduce energy consumption from your refrigerator's ice maker, follow these tips:
1. **Clean the ice maker regularly** to ensure it's working efficiently.
2. **Defrost the ice maker** every 6 months to remove any built-up ice.
3. Check your refrigerator's temperature settings to ensure they're within the recommended range.
According to [Energy Star](https://www.energystar.gov/), a well-maintained refrigerator can save up to **15%** on energy consumption.
