## Measuring Your Sink for the Perfect Fit
To ensure a perfect fit for your over sink shelf organizer, follow these steps:
1. Measure the width of your sink: Use a tape measure to determine the width of your sink, taking into account any obstructions such as faucets or soap dispensers.
2. Measure the depth of your sink: Measure the depth of your sink to determine the maximum depth of the over sink shelf organizer.
3. Check the height: Measure the height of your sink and the surrounding area to determine the ideal height for your over sink shelf organizer.
