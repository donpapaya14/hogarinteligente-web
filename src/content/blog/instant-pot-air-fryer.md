## Tips and Tricks for Using Instant Pot Air Fryer
To get the most out of the Instant Pot Air Fryer, follow these tips and tricks:
1. **Preheat the air fryer** before cooking to ensure even cooking.
2. **Pat dry food** before cooking to prevent steam from building up.
3. **Use the right cooking time and temperature** for your specific food.
4. **Shake the basket** halfway through cooking to ensure even cooking.
5. **Clean the air fryer regularly** to prevent food residue from building up.
