## The 15-Minute Perfect Rice Formula
Achieving perfect rice is all about the **water ratio**. For **jasmine rice**, use a **1:1.2** ratio, for **basmati rice**, use **1:1.5**, and for **brown rice**, use **1:2.2**, according to the [Zojirushi Rice Cooking Manual](https://www.zojirushi.com) 2022. You can also use the **'finger rule'** for no-measure cooking: the water level should be at the first knuckle of your index finger.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best small rice cooker for beginners?
The best small rice cooker for beginners is one that is easy to use and clean, with features like **auto-shutoff** and **keep-warm**. The [Aroma Housewares ARC-914SBD Digital Rice Cooker](https://www.amazon.com/Aroma-Housewares-ARC-914SBD-Digital-Cooker) is a great option.
### Can I cook other grains in a rice cooker?
Yes, you can cook other grains like **quinoa**, **oatmeal**, and **barley** in a rice cooker. Just adjust the **water ratio** and **cooking time** according to the grain's instructions.
### How do I clean my rice cooker?
To clean your rice cooker, unplug it and let it cool. Then, wash the **inner pot** and **accessories** with soap and water, and dry thoroughly. For tough stains, mix **baking soda** and **water** to create a paste, and apply it to the stain.
### What are the health benefits of using a rice cooker?
Using a rice cooker can help you cook **healthy meals** with ease, as it allows for **precise temperature control** and **even cooking**. This can help retain more **nutrients** in your food, according to the [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int).
### Can I use a rice cooker to cook frozen vegetables?
Yes, you can use a rice cooker to cook **frozen vegetables**. Just add the frozen veggies to the cooker with some **water** or **broth**, and cook until they're tender.
### How do I store my rice cooker when not in use?
To store your rice cooker when not in use, make sure it's **completely dry** and **clean**. Then, store it in a **dry place**, away from **direct sunlight** and **moisture**.
