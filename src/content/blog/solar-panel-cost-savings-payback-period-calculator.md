## Understanding the Solar Panel Payback Period
The **solar panel payback period** refers to the time it takes for the savings from a solar panel system to equal the cost of the system. According to the [National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)](https://www.nrel.gov), the payback period is calculated by dividing the total cost of the system by the annual savings. Factors affecting the payback period include **solar panel efficiency**, **installation costs**, and **electricity prices**.

## Federal and Local Tax Credits for Solar Panels
The federal government offers a **26% tax credit** for solar panel installations, known as the [Investment Tax Credit (ITC)](https://www.energy.gov/eere/solar/solar-energy-tax-credits). Additionally, many states and local governments offer **utility rebates** and **property tax exemptions**. The [Database of State Incentives for Renewables & Efficiency (DSIRE)](https://www.dsireusa.org) provides a comprehensive list of available incentives.
