## Understanding the 'Drawer Paradox' and its Impact on Kitchen Efficiency
The **kitchen drawer organization system** is a crucial aspect of kitchen efficiency, as a study by the [American Culinary Federation (ACF)](https://www.acfchefs.org/) in 2020 found that the average home cook spends **12 minutes** per day searching for kitchen utensils. This **'Drawer Paradox'** affects cooking time and overall kitchen productivity.

## Step 1: Purge and Categorize Kitchen Utensils
To start, purge your kitchen utensils and categorize them into groups, such as **baking**, **cooking**, and **serving**. According to a guide by the [National Kitchen and Bath Association (NKBA)](https://www.nkba.org/), essential kitchen utensils to keep include:
* 1 **chef's knife**
* 1 **cutting board**
* 1 **set of measuring cups**
* 1 **set of measuring spoons**
* 1 **can opener**
