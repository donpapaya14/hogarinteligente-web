## Introduction to Alexa Routine for Smart Lighting Control
An **Alexa routine for smart lighting control** is a customizable sequence of actions that can be triggered by a voice command, schedule, or smart device. According to [Amazon Documentation (2022)](https://developer.amazon.com), Alexa routines can be used to control various smart devices, including lights, thermostats, and security cameras.

## What is an Alexa Routine and How Does it Work?
An Alexa routine is a set of actions that can be triggered by a single voice command or schedule. For example, you can create a routine that turns on the lights, adjusts the thermostat, and locks the doors when you leave the house. To create an Alexa routine, follow these steps:
1. Open the Alexa app and navigate to the Routines section.
2. Click on the + icon to create a new routine.
3. Choose the trigger for the routine, such as a voice command or schedule.
4. Add the actions you want to include in the routine, such as turning on the lights or adjusting the thermostat.
