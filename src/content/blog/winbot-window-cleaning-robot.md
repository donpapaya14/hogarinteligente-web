## Introduction to Winbot

The **Winbot window cleaning robot** is an **automated window cleaning** solution designed to simplify the tedious task of window maintenance. Developed by Ecovacs Robotics, this **smart window cleaner** uses advanced sensors and AI to navigate and clean glass surfaces efficiently. According to [Ecovacs Robotics (2022)](https://www.ecovacs.com), the Winbot series can reduce cleaning time by **70%** compared to manual methods, while achieving a streak-free finish. Key features include:

- **Automatic path planning** for complete coverage
- **Strong suction** (up to 4,500 Pa) for secure attachment
- **Quiet operation** (below 65 dB)
- **App control** via smartphone for scheduling

## How Winbot Works

The **window washing robot** follows a **4-step process** ([IEEE Robotics Journal, 2020](https://www.ieee.org)):

1. **Attachment**: Uses vacuum suction to secure itself to the window
2. **Mapping**: Scans the surface with LiDAR to create a cleaning path
3. **Cleaning**: Moves in a systematic N-pattern while spraying and wiping
4. **Completion**: Returns to starting point and detaches automatically

## Maintenance and Troubleshooting

Per [Ecovacs Support (2023)](https://support.ecovacs.com), follow these steps:

1. **Weekly**: Rinse microfiber pads with warm water (no detergent)
2. **Monthly**: Check suction motor filter for debris
3. **Quarterly**: Calibrate sensors via app

Common issues and fixes:

- **Error 05**: Clean boundary sensors with dry cloth
- **Weak suction**: Replace filter (part #WB-FF01) every 12 months
- **App disconnect**: Reset Wi-Fi module (hold power 10 seconds)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Winbot take to clean a standard window?
A **3x4 ft window takes 8-12 minutes** with the Winbot 950. Larger surfaces (6x8 ft) require 25 minutes according to Ecovacs lab tests (2023).

### Can Winbot clean outside windows?
Yes, but **only on ground-floor windows** with safety tether attached. The robot isn't rated for heights above 10ft without professional installation.

### What surfaces can't Winbot clean?
Avoid **textured glass, mirrors under 3mm thick, or windows with heavy grime buildup**. The [IEEE study](https://www.ieee.org) notes 92% effectiveness drops to 65% on non-smooth surfaces.

### How often should I replace Winbot parts?

- **Battery**: 18-24 months ($49)

### Is Winbot worth the investment?
