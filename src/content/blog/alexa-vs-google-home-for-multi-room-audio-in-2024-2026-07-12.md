## True Multi-Room Sync Performance
According to [RTINGS.com](https://www.rtings.com) smart speaker tests (2023), **Alexa** and **Google Home** have different latency measurements. Alexa averages around **35 ms** between rooms, while Google Home averages **29 ms**. Additionally, Google Home supports up to **100 speaker groups**, whereas Alexa supports up to **50 groups**. However, both platforms can experience **audio drift issues**.
