## Choosing the Right Under-Bed Organizers
When selecting under-bed organizers, consider key features such as low profile, durability, and whether wheels are necessary. A comparison between fabric and plastic bins reveals that fabric bins are more flexible and can be compressed to fit into tighter spaces, while plastic bins offer better protection against moisture and pests.

## Step-by-Step Organization System
To categorize items for under-bed storage:
1. Sort items into categories (e.g., out-of-season clothes, bedding, shoes).
2. Purge items that are no longer needed or useful.
3. Assign a home for each item under the bed using bins or containers.
4. Label each bin for easy access.
