## Introduction to Thermostat Energy Saving Settings
The optimal **thermostat energy saving settings** can significantly reduce your monthly energy bills. According to the [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), heating and cooling systems account for nearly half of the average household's energy consumption. 

## The Impact of Temperature on Energy Consumption
The temperature setting on your thermostat has a direct impact on your energy consumption. The concept of 'temperature lift' refers to the increase in energy consumption as the temperature difference between the inside and outside of your home increases. A study by the [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov) (2019) found that for every degree of temperature lift, energy consumption increases by **3-5%**. 

## Recommended Temperature Settings for Energy Savings
The ideal temperature settings for energy savings vary depending on the time of day and year. According to a study by the [National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)](https://www.nrel.gov) (2015), the optimal temperature range for winter is between **68-70°F** and for summer is between **78-80°F**. Here are some recommended temperature settings:
* Winter: 68°F during the day and 65°F at night
* Summer: 78°F during the day and 82°F at night
These settings can result in an average monthly energy savings of **$20-30**.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Setting Your Thermostat
One common mistake people make when setting their thermostats is setting it too low in winter or too high in summer. According to the [American Society of Heating, Refrigerating, and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE)](https://www.ashrae.org) (2020), this can result in increased energy consumption and higher bills. Here are some tips to avoid these mistakes:
1. Set your thermostat to the optimal temperature range
2. Use a smart thermostat to automate temperature adjustments
3. Adjust your thermostat settings based on the time of day and year
