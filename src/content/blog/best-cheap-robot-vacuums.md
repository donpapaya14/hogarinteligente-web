### What’s the cheapest reliable robot vacuum?
The **Eufy RoboVac 11** at $149 is the most reliable budget option, with **4.3/5 stars** from 12,000+ Amazon reviews. It outperforms pricier models in [Wirecutter’s 2023](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter) tests.

### Do cheap robot vacuums work on carpets?
Yes, but performance varies. Models with **≥1000Pa suction** (like the **Lefant M210**) clean low-pile carpets effectively, but may struggle with shag rugs per [Consumer Reports 2023](https://www.consumerreports.org).

### How often should I clean my robot vacuum?
Empty the dustbin **after each use** and clean brushes **weekly** to maintain suction. [iFixit 2022](https://www.ifixit.com) found this extends lifespan by **30%**.

### Can robot vacuums replace traditional vacuums?
For daily maintenance, yes. But deep cleaning still requires upright vacuums **1-2x/month**, especially for carpets, as per [CNET 2023](https://www.cnet.com).

### Are refurbished robot vacuums worth it?
Only if certified (e.g., Amazon Renewed). [Trustpilot 2024](https://www.trustpilot.com) shows **22% failure rates** for non-certified refurbished units vs. 8% for new.
