## Why Most Grout Cleaners Fail
The **pH balance** of a grout cleaner is crucial for its effectiveness. A study by the Good Housekeeping Institute Cleaning Lab (2023) found that cleaners with a pH level closer to that of grout itself are more effective. For instance, a cleaner with a pH level of around 7 is more likely to be effective than one with a highly alkaline or acidic pH.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best grout cleaner for tile floors?
The best grout cleaner for tile floors depends on the type of tile and the level of grime. For most tile floors, **Laticrete Grout & Tile Cleaner** is a top choice due to its effectiveness and gentle pH balance.
### How often should I clean my grout?
Cleaning grout regularly can prevent the buildup of dirt and grime. It's recommended to clean grout at least once a month, or more often in high-traffic areas.
### Can I use bleach to clean grout?
While bleach can be effective against certain types of stains, it's not recommended for regular grout cleaning due to its harsh nature. Instead, opt for a pH-balanced cleaner.
### What is the best way to prevent grout stains?
Preventing grout stains involves regular cleaning and sealing of the grout. Applying a grout sealer can help protect the grout from stains and make cleaning easier.
### How do I remove old grout?
Removing old grout requires patience and the right tools. Start by using a grout saw or a rotary tool with a grout removal attachment to carefully scrape out the old grout.
### Can I use a steam cleaner on all types of grout?
Not all types of grout are suitable for steam cleaning. It's essential to check the manufacturer's recommendations before using a steam cleaner to avoid damaging the grout or surrounding tiles.
