## Designing Your 30-Minute Kitchen Prep Routine
To create a personalized **kitchen prep routine**, follow these steps:
1. Set a timer for **30 minutes**.
2. Divide the time into **5-minute** intervals.
3. Assign a task to each interval, such as:
   * Interval 1: Wash and chop vegetables
   * Interval 2: Prepare protein sources
   * Interval 3: Cook grains or starches
   * Interval 4: Assemble meals
   * Interval 5: Clean and sanitize the kitchen
   * Interval 6: Label and store leftovers

According to the National Kitchen and Bath Association (2022), a well-designed **kitchen prep routine** can increase productivity and reduce stress.

## Common Label Maker Mistakes to Avoid
When using a **label maker** in the kitchen, avoid the following common mistakes:
* Using a font size that is too small
* Selecting a font color that does not contrast with the label background
* Not testing the label maker before use

According to Label Maker Forums (2023), these mistakes can lead to labels that are difficult to read or do not adhere properly.
