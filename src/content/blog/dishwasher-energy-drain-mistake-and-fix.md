## The Hidden Energy Drain: Rinse Hold Setting

Using the 'Rinse Hold' or 'Delay Start' feature on your dishwasher may seem convenient, but it can significantly increase energy use without most users realizing it. According to the ENERGY STAR Appliance Usage Study 2022, this feature keeps water heated for hours, wasting energy that could be saved.

### How It Works

The 'Rinse Hold' feature is designed to keep the water heated for a set period, usually 1-2 hours, allowing you to run a cycle at a later time. However, this feature is often overlooked, and users may not realize the impact it has on their energy consumption.

To put this into perspective, let's break down the kWh usage and cost per cycle when using Rinse Hold vs normal cycle. ### The Sensor Wash Overload Problem

Another common mistake is overloading the dishwasher. This triggers more water heating, as the sensors think the dishes are dirtier. According to the Consumer Reports Dishwasher Loading Guide 2021, optimal loading diagrams can help you avoid this problem.

### Better Alternatives to Rinse Hold

If you're looking for a more energy-efficient option, consider using the 'Quick Wash' feature with air dry. This can save you up to 20% on energy consumption. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started:

1. Choose the 'Quick Wash' cycle
2. Select the 'Air Dry' option
3. Run the cycle as usual

### Pro Maintenance Tip Most Miss

Cleaning the hidden filter monthly can improve efficiency by 15%. To do this:

1. Locate the filter, usually found at the bottom of the dishwasher
2. Remove any large debris
3. Soak the filter in hot soapy water for 10 minutes
4. Rinse and replace the filter
