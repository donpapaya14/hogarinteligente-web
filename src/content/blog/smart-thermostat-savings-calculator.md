## Introduction to Smart Thermostat Energy Savings
A smart thermostat can save the average household up to **$150 per year** on energy bills, according to a study by the [U.S. Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), 2019. This is because smart thermostats can learn your schedule and preferences to optimize heating and cooling usage. For example, the [Nest Learning Thermostat (3rd Generation)](Nest Learning Thermostat) can automatically adjust the temperature when you're away from home.

## How Much Energy Can a Smart Thermostat Save You?
The U.S. Department of Energy study found that households can save between **10-30%** on their energy bills by using a smart thermostat. This is because smart thermostats can:
* Learn your schedule and preferences
* Automatically adjust the temperature when you're away from home
* Provide detailed energy usage reports to help you identify areas for improvement
According to the [Smart Home Alliance](https://www.smarthomealliance.org), 2022, the key to maximizing energy savings is to choose a smart thermostat that is compatible with your HVAC system and has features such as geofencing and remote access.

## Factors to Consider When Choosing a Smart Thermostat
When choosing a smart thermostat, consider the following key features:
1. **Compatibility**: Ensure the thermostat is compatible with your HVAC system
2. **Geofencing**: Look for thermostats with geofencing capabilities to automatically adjust the temperature when you're away from home
3. **Remote access**: Choose a thermostat with remote access through a mobile app to adjust the temperature and monitor energy usage on-the-go
The [Smart Home Alliance](https://www.smarthomealliance.org), 2022, recommends considering these factors to ensure you choose the best smart thermostat for your needs.

## Smart Thermostat Maintenance and Troubleshooting
To ensure your smart thermostat is working efficiently, follow these maintenance and troubleshooting tips:
1. **Check for software updates**: Regularly check for software updates to ensure your thermostat is running the latest version
2. **Clean the thermostat**: Clean the thermostat regularly to ensure it is working accurately
3. **Check for connectivity issues**: Check for connectivity issues with your Wi-Fi network or mobile app
By following these tips, you can ensure your smart thermostat is working efficiently and effectively.
