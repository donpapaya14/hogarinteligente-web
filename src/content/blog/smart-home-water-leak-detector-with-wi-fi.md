## Introduction to Smart Home Water Leak Detectors
A **smart home water leak detector with Wi-Fi** is a device that uses sensors to detect water leaks in your home and sends alerts to your smartphone or tablet via Wi-Fi. According to the [National Institute of Building Sciences](https://www.nibs.org), 2020, these devices can help prevent water damage and reduce insurance claims.

## What is a Smart Home Water Leak Detector and How Does it Work?
A smart home water leak detector typically consists of a sensor that detects water leaks and a Wi-Fi module that sends alerts to your device. The sensor can be placed near water pipes, appliances, or in areas prone to flooding. When a leak is detected, the sensor sends a signal to the Wi-Fi module, which then sends an alert to your device. For example, the [Sensi Smart Water Leak Detector with Wi-Fi](https://www.amazon.com) (around $50) can be placed near your water heater or washing machine to detect leaks.
