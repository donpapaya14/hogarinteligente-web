## Introduction to Blender Blade Sharpening Mistakes
The **blender blade sharpening mistake** that dulls blades is a common issue many kitchen enthusiasts face. According to a study by the [Journal of Food Engineering](https://www.jfoodeng.com), **crushing ice** can create micro-fractures in blade edges, leading to a significant decrease in blade lifespan.

## The Wrong Way Most People 'Sharpen' Blades
The rice/eggshell cleaning method is a common **blender maintenance tip**, but it can actually **round blade edges**, making them less sharp. According to [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org) Kitchen Tools Testing in 2023, this method can lead to a **30% decrease** in blade sharpness.

## Professional Sharpening Method That Works
To properly sharpen your blender blades, use a **diamond honing rod** at a **22° angle**. Here are the steps:
1. Hold the rod at a 22° angle.
2. Slowly move the rod along the blade edge.
3. Repeat the process 2-3 times.
As recommended by [WÜSTHOF](https://www.wusthof.com) Knife Sharpening Guidelines in 2022, this method can increase blade sharpness by **40%**.

## When to Replace vs. Sharpen
It's essential to know when to replace or sharpen your blender blades. If the blade has **chips >2mm**, it's best to replace it. However, if the wear is salvageable, sharpening can extend the blade's lifespan. According to a [Vitamix](https://www.vitamix.com) Blade Replacement Threshold Study in 2020, **60%** of blender blades can be sharpened instead of replaced.
