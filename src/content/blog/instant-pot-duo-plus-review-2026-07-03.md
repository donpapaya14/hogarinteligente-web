## What is Instant Pot Duo Plus?

According to the [Instant Pot Official Website (2022)](https://www.instantpot.com), the Duo Plus model includes **11 smart programs** (e.g., Soup, Meat/Stew, Cake) and a **3D heating system** for even cooking. - **Pressure cooking**: Cuts cooking time by up to 70% (e.g., 8-hour stew in 35 minutes)

## Cooking with Instant Pot Duo Plus

**Step-by-step chicken and rice recipe** (adapted from *The Instant Pot Electric Pressure Cooker Cookbook, 2019*):

1. **Sauté**: Heat 1 tbsp oil using the Sauté function. Add 1 lb diced chicken (5 minutes).
2. **Add ingredients**: Pour 1 cup rinsed rice, 1.5 cups broth, and spices.
3. **Pressure cook**: Seal lid, select High Pressure for 5 minutes.
4. **Release**: Wait 10-minute natural release, then quick release remaining pressure.

Total time: **22 minutes** (vs. 45 minutes stovetop).
