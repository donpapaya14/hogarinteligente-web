## Why Christmas Lights Can Be a Safety Hazard

According to the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) 2020 study on holiday lighting safety, Christmas lights are a leading cause of home fires during the holiday season. In fact, the NFPA reports that between 2013 and 2017, an estimated 43 home fires per year were caused by holiday lights, resulting in an average of 13 civilian deaths, 26 civilian injuries, and $16 million in direct property damage annually.

The primary cause of these fires is due to the improper use of traditional Christmas lights, which can overheat and ignite flammable materials. To mitigate this risk, homeowners can consider using smart plugs for their Christmas lights.
