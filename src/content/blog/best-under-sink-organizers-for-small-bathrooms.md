## Why Standard Organizers Fail Under Sinks

Under-sink organizers for small bathrooms often fail because of irregular plumbing configurations. The **P-trap and supply lines** consume up to 40% of usable space, according to the [National Kitchen & Bath Association 2023 guidelines](https://nkba.org). To measure correctly:

1. Measure the **height** from the cabinet floor to the bottom of the sink (average: 18-24")
2. Note the **depth** behind plumbing (typically 8-12")
3. Account for **door clearance** (minimum 1" for sliding systems)
