## Introduction to Optimal Central Heating Temperature in Summer
The **optimal central heating temperature in summer** is crucial for reducing energy consumption and minimizing carbon footprint. According to the [International Energy Agency (2020)](https://www.iea.org), adjusting central heating temperature settings can lead to significant energy savings. In this article, we will explore the importance of adjusting central heating temperature in summer, recommended temperature settings, and practical tips for maintaining a comfortable temperature.

## Why Lowering Central Heating Temperature in Summer Matters
Lowering central heating temperature in summer can have a significant impact on energy consumption and carbon footprint. A study by the [International Energy Agency (2020)](https://www.iea.org) found that a 1°C reduction in temperature can lead to a **5-10% reduction in energy consumption**. This can result in significant cost savings and a reduced carbon footprint.

## The Ideal Central Heating Temperature for Summer
The recommended temperature setting for central heating in summer varies depending on the source. According to the [Energy Savings Trust (2019)](https://www.energysavingtrust.org.uk), the ideal temperature setting is between **19-21°C**. However, this can vary depending on individual preferences and the specific heating system.

| Temperature Setting | Energy Consumption |
| --- | --- |
| 20°C | 100% |

## Adjusting Temperature Settings for Specific Rooms
Adjusting temperature settings for specific rooms can also lead to energy savings. According to [British Gas (2020)](https://www.britishgas.co.uk), the following temperature settings are recommended:
1. **Kitchen: 18-20°C**
2. **Living Room: 19-21°C**
3. **Bedroom: 16-18°C**
