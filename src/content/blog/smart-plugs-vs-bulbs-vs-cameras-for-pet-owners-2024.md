## Setup Tips to Avoid Common Pet Hazards
To avoid common pet hazards, follow these setup tips:
1. Secure cords and place devices out of reach.
2. Use pet-friendly voice commands (Alexa/Google).
3. Place cameras at a safe distance to avoid accidents.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the best smart devices for pet owners?
The best smart devices for pet owners include smart plugs, smart bulbs, and pet cameras. These devices can help automate feeding, reduce pet stress, and provide security and interaction features.
### How do smart plugs work for pet feeders?
Smart plugs work by automating pet feeders, ensuring your pet is fed at the right time. They can be controlled remotely using a smartphone app.
### What are the benefits of color-changing bulbs for pets?
Color-changing bulbs can help reduce pet stress and anxiety. They can be scheduled to mimic sunrise and sunset, creating a natural environment for your pet.
