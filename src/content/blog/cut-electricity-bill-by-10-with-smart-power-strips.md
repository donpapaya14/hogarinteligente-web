## Calculating Energy Savings with Smart Power Strips
To calculate energy savings with smart power strips, follow these steps:
1. Determine your average energy consumption per device
2. Calculate the number of hours per day each device is used
3. Multiply the energy consumption per device by the number of hours used
4. Compare the results with and without using smart power strips
