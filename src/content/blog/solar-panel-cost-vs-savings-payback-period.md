## Calculating the Payback Period of Solar Panels
The payback period of solar panels can be calculated by dividing the total cost of the system by the annual energy savings. According to the [NREL (National Renewable Energy Laboratory) 2020 study](https://www.nrel.gov), the payback period can range from **5 to 15 years**, depending on the location and the cost of electricity. Here is a step-by-step guide to calculate the payback period:

1. Determine the total cost of the solar panel system.
2. Calculate the annual energy savings by multiplying the system size by the average annual sunlight hours and the cost of electricity.
3. Divide the total cost by the annual energy savings to get the payback period.
