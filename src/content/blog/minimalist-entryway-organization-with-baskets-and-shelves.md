## Optimizing Entryway Shelving for Maximum Storage
To design and install the perfect entryway shelving system, consider the size and shape of your entryway, as well as the type of items you want to store. According to [The Spruce's Guide to Entryway Shelving (2020)](https://www.thespruce.com), **80%** of homeowners prefer floating shelves for a minimalist look. Here are some expert tips:

1. Measure your entryway space carefully to determine the optimal shelf size and placement.
2. Choose shelf materials that are durable and easy to clean, such as wood or metal.
3. Consider installing a shelf with a built-in hook or basket for storing keys, hats, and other small items.

## Implementing a Maintenance Routine to Keep Your Entryway Organized
To maintain your entryway's organization, create a daily and weekly routine that includes tasks such as:

1. Wipe down shelves and storage containers with a damp cloth.
2. Put away items that are out of place, such as shoes or keys.
3. Vacuum or sweep the entryway floor to keep it clean and clutter-free.
