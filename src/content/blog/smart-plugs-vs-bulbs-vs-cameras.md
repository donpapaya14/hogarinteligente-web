## Introduction to Smart Home Devices
When it comes to **smart home devices comparison**, there are several options to consider, including smart plugs, bulbs, and cameras. According to [SmartThings](https://www.smartthings.com), smart home devices can enhance the convenience, security, and energy efficiency of a home.

## What is the Difference Between Smart Plugs, Bulbs, and Cameras?
Smart plugs, bulbs, and cameras are all **smart home devices** that serve different purposes. Smart plugs are used to control and monitor the energy usage of appliances, while smart bulbs are used for lighting and can be controlled remotely. Smart cameras, on the other hand, are used for security and surveillance. As stated by [Smart Home Devices 101 by SmartThings](https://www.smartthings.com), these devices can be integrated into a smart home system to enhance their functionality.
