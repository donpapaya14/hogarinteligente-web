## Types of Over the Door Storage Racks
There are several types of **over the door storage racks**, including hook-style, shelf-style, and pocket-style racks. A comparison of these types is provided below:
| Type | Description | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook-style | Hooks for hanging items | Easy to install, versatile |
| Shelf-style | Shelves for storing items | Provides additional storage space |
| Pocket-style | Pockets for storing small items | Keeps items organized and out of sight |
As noted by [The Spruce](https://www.thespruce.com), the choice of rack depends on individual needs and preferences.

## Measuring Your Door for the Perfect Fit
To ensure the perfect fit for your **over the door storage rack**, follow these steps:
1. Measure the width of your door.
2. Measure the height of your door.
3. Check the weight capacity of the rack.
According to [This Old House](https://www.thisoldhouse.com), these measurements are crucial for a secure and functional installation.
