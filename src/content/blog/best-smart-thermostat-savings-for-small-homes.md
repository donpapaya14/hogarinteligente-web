## Smart Thermostat Installation and Maintenance
To install a smart thermostat, follow these steps:
1. Turn off the power to the thermostat at the circuit breaker.
2. Remove the old thermostat.
3. Install the new thermostat.
4. Connect the wires to the new thermostat.
5. Turn on the power to the thermostat.
For maintenance, check the thermostat's filters every **3-6 months** and replace them as needed.

## Temperature Control Strategies for Small Homes
To optimize temperature control in small homes, use these strategies:
1. **Zoning**: Divide the home into different zones, each with its own thermostat.
2. **Scheduling**: Set the thermostat to adjust the temperature based on the home's schedule.
3. **Setback**: Set the thermostat to lower the temperature when the home is unoccupied.
According to the [National Association of Home Builders](https://www.nahb.org/), these strategies can save small homes up to **30%** on heating and cooling costs.
