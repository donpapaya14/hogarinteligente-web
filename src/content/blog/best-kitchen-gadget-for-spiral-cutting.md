## What is Spiral Cutting and Why is it Important?
Spiral cutting is a technique used to cut vegetables and fruits into long, curly strips, increasing their surface area and making them more visually appealing. According to the [Harvard School of Public Health](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu), 2019, spiral cutting can help retain more nutrients in vegetables, as it reduces the amount of heat and water used in cooking. For example, a study published in the [Journal of Food Science](https://ift.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17503841) found that spiral-cut carrots retained **25% more** vitamin C than sliced carrots.
