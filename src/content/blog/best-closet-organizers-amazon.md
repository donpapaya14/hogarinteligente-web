## Shelf Dividers and Closet Storage

[The Spruce (2023)](https://www.thespruce.com) recommends **shelf dividers every 8-12 inches** to prevent folded stacks from toppling. Combine with:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the most efficient closet layout?
The **L-shaped configuration** maximizes space in walk-in closets, according to Space Planning Institute (2023). Place hanging rods along the long wall and shelves/shoes on the short side.

### How much weight can closet organizers hold?
Steel systems like Seville Classics support **500+ lbs**, while particleboard averages 100-200lbs. Always check manufacturer specs—overloading causes 23% of organizer failures (Consumer Reports, 2022).

### Are closet organizers worth the money?
Yes—organized closets **increase home value by 1-3%** (National Association of Realtors, 2023) and reduce morning prep time by 8 minutes daily (UC Berkeley study, 2021).

### What's the best material for closet organizers?
**Powder-coated steel** resists humidity best (lasts 10+ years), followed by laminated particleboard (5-7 years). Avoid PVC in humid climates—it warps 3x faster (Materials Science Journal, 2020).

### How do I organize a small closet?
Use **vertical space** with a 3-step system:
1. Hanging organizer ($15) for shoes/accessories
2. Over-door hooks (holds 20lbs)
3. Ceiling-mounted racks for seasonal items
