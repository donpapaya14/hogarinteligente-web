## Introduction to Smart Thermostats for Small Homes
For **2-4 person households**, installing a smart thermostat can lead to significant energy savings. According to a study by the [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), 2019, households with smart thermostats can save an average of **$130-$145** per year.

## How Much Can Smart Thermostats Really Save?
The savings from smart thermostats come from their ability to learn and adapt to a household's schedule and preferences. A study by the [National Resources Defense Council](https://www.nrdc.org) found that smart thermostats can reduce energy consumption by **10-12%**. This is because they can automatically adjust the temperature when the house is empty or when the occupants are sleeping.

## Smart Thermostat Features to Consider for Small Households
Some key features to look for in a smart thermostat include:
1. **Geofencing**: This feature allows the thermostat to detect when you are away from home and adjust the temperature accordingly.
2. **Scheduling**: This feature allows you to set a schedule for your thermostat to follow, so you can save energy when you're not home.
3. **Energy monitoring**: This feature allows you to track your energy usage and see where you can make adjustments to save more.

## Smart Thermostat Installation and Maintenance Tips
To get the most out of your smart thermostat, it's essential to install and maintain it properly. Here are some tips:
1. **Follow the manufacturer's instructions** for installation.
2. **Regularly update your thermostat's software** to ensure you have the latest features and security patches.
3. **Check your thermostat's batteries** regularly to ensure they are not running low.
