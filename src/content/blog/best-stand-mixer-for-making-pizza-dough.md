## Understanding the Science of Pizza Dough
The role of **yeast** in creating the perfect pizza dough cannot be overstated. According to a study published in [The American Journal of Food Science](https://www.jfoodscience.org), 2020, yeast fermentation is responsible for the development of **gluten**, which gives pizza dough its chewy texture. Temperature control is also crucial, with optimal yeast activation occurring between **25-30°C**.

## Tips for Making Perfect Pizza Dough
To make perfect pizza dough, follow these expert tips:
1. **Temperature control**: Keep the dough at a consistent temperature between **25-30°C**.
2. **Yeast activation**: Use **warm water** (around **37°C**) to activate the yeast.
3. **Kneading techniques**: Use a combination of **stretching** and **folding** to develop the gluten.
