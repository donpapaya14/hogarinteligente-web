## Introduction to OxiClean and Hydrogen Peroxide Risks
Mixing **OxiClean and Hydrogen Peroxide** can be extremely dangerous due to the exothermic reaction that occurs, releasing oxygen gas and heat. According to the [Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)](https://www.cpsc.gov), both products are commonly used for cleaning purposes but should be handled with caution.

## What is OxiClean and Hydrogen Peroxide?
**OxiClean** is a popular cleaning product containing sodium percarbonate, which is a combination of sodium carbonate and hydrogen peroxide. **Hydrogen Peroxide**, on the other hand, is a strong oxidizer used in various household and industrial applications. Both products have their own set of uses and guidelines for safe handling, as outlined by the [Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)](https://www.cpsc.gov) guidelines.

## The Chemical Reaction Behind the Danger
When **OxiClean and Hydrogen Peroxide** are combined, they undergo an exothermic reaction, releasing oxygen gas and heat. This reaction can lead to the formation of toxic fumes and increase the risk of fire or explosion. A [National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH)](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/) study on chemical reactions highlights the importance of handling these substances with care.

## Symptoms of Exposure to the Toxic Fumes
Exposure to the toxic fumes resulting from the mixture of **OxiClean and Hydrogen Peroxide** can cause severe health effects, including:
* Respiratory issues
* Skin irritation
* Eye damage
According to the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)](https://www.cdc.gov), it is crucial to seek medical attention immediately if any symptoms occur.

## Preventing Accidents and Injuries
To prevent accidents and injuries, it is essential to follow safe handling and storage practices for **OxiClean and Hydrogen Peroxide**. The [American Cleaning Institute (ACI)](https://www.cleaninginstitute.org) recommends:
1. Using protective gear, such as gloves and goggles
2. Ensuring proper ventilation when using these products
3. Storing them in a cool, dry place, away from other chemicals

## Treatment and First Aid for Exposure
If exposure to the toxic fumes occurs, it is vital to provide first aid and emergency treatment. The [American Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org) guidelines on treating chemical exposure recommend:
* Moving the affected individual to fresh air
* Removing contaminated clothing and washing the skin with soap and water
* Seeking medical attention immediately
