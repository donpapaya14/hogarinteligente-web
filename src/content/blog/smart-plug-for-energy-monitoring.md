## Introduction to Smart Plugs
A **smart plug energy monitoring** system can help you reduce your electricity bill by up to **23%**, according to the [U.S. Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), 2020. Smart plugs are devices that can be plugged into a wall outlet and then have a device, such as a lamp or television, plugged into them. They can be controlled remotely using a smartphone app and can also track and report energy usage.

## How Smart Plugs Monitor Energy
Smart plugs use **advanced sensors** to track and report energy usage in real-time, as stated in the IEEE Journal of Smart Grid, 2019. They can also provide **detailed analytics** on energy consumption patterns, allowing users to identify areas where they can reduce their energy usage.

### Technical Details
The technical details of how smart plugs monitor energy are as follows:
* They use **Wi-Fi or Bluetooth** to connect to the internet and communicate with the user's smartphone app.
* They have **built-in sensors** that track the amount of energy being used by the device plugged into them.
* They can provide **real-time energy usage data**, allowing users to monitor their energy consumption and make adjustments as needed.

## Setting Up Your Smart Plug
Setting up a smart plug is a straightforward process that can be completed in a few steps:
1. Plug the smart plug into a wall outlet.
2. Download and install the smartphone app for the smart plug.
3. Follow the in-app instructions to connect the smart plug to your Wi-Fi network.
4. Plug a device, such as a lamp or television, into the smart plug.
5. Use the app to control and monitor the device.

## Using Data to Reduce Energy Consumption
Using the data provided by a smart plug, users can take steps to reduce their energy consumption, such as:
* Identifying devices that are using the most energy and replacing them with more energy-efficient models.
* Scheduling devices to turn off when not in use.
* Adjusting usage patterns to reduce peak demand.
According to [Energy Star](https://www.energystar.gov/), 2020, using energy-efficient devices and adjusting usage patterns can reduce energy consumption by up to **30%**.
