## Measuring and Installing Shelf Dividers

Follow these steps to **install closet shelf dividers** correctly:

1. **Measure your shelf depth and width** using a tape measure. Most dividers fit shelves 12–24 inches deep.
2. **Choose dividers with adjustable widths** if your closet dimensions vary. The [Seville Classics Divider](AMAZON:Seville Classics Closet Shelf Divider) expands up to 24 inches.
3. **Place dividers 6–8 inches apart** for folded clothes, or 4–5 inches for shoes (per [The Spruce, 2022](https://www.thespruce.com)).
4. **Secure dividers** with adhesive strips or screws, depending on the model.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do closet shelf dividers work?
Closet shelf dividers create vertical partitions on shelves to separate items like clothes or shoes. They prevent stacks from collapsing and maximize space efficiency.

### What’s the best material for shelf dividers?
Plastic and metal dividers are lightweight and affordable, while wooden dividers offer sturdiness for heavier items.

### Can I use shelf dividers for shoes?
Yes. Space them 4–5 inches apart to keep pairs upright, as recommended by [Real Simple (2021)](https://www.realsimple.com).

### How many dividers do I need per shelf?
For folded clothes, use 2–3 dividers per 24-inch shelf. For shoes, 4–5 dividers work best.

### Are DIY shelf dividers effective?
Yes, but they may lack durability. Cardboard dividers last 6–12 months, while wooden DIY options can last years.
