## Introduction to the Best Garlic Press for Arthritic Hands
The **best garlic press for arthritis** is designed to alleviate the strain on joints, making cooking easier for individuals with arthritic hands. According to the [Arthritis Foundation](https://www.arthritis.org), grip strength requirements in traditional garlic presses can exacerbate joint pain, typically requiring **20-30 lbs of force**.

## Why Standard Garlic Presses Hurt Arthritic Hands
Traditional garlic presses can be challenging for individuals with arthritic hands due to the high grip strength required. The [Arthritis Foundation 2022 grip strength guidelines](https://www.arthritis.org) recommend considering tools that reduce the need for high-force gripping. This is where **ergonomic garlic presses** come into play, offering a solution that is both easy to use and gentle on the hands.

## Key Features for Arthritis-Friendly Designs
When looking for an **easy-squeeze garlic tool**, there are three must-have features to consider:
1. **Lever mechanics** that reduce the force needed to crush garlic.
2. **Non-slip handles** for a secure grip, even with wet or oily hands.
3. **One-handed operation capabilities** for maximum convenience and minimal strain.

As highlighted in the [Journal of Hand Therapy 2021 ergonomic tool study](https://www.jhandtherapy.org), these features are crucial for individuals with arthritis, enhancing usability and comfort.
