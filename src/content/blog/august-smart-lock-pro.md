The **August Smart Lock Pro** is a **keyless entry** system that replaces your traditional deadbolt, offering **smart home** integration and enhanced security. According to [August's official website](https://august.com), it features **Auto-Unlock**, **Wi-Fi connectivity**, and compatibility with **Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit**. A 2021 Yale University study on smart lock adoption found that **62% of users reported improved home security** after switching to keyless systems.

Key benefits include:
1. **Remote access**: Control your lock from anywhere via the August app
2. **Guest access**: Create virtual keys with expiration dates
3. **Activity log**: Track who enters and exits your home

From [August's support site](https://support.august.com):
1. **Battery issues**: Replace batteries when app shows <20% (every 5-6 months)
2. **Connection drops**: Reset Wi-Fi bridge by holding button for 10 seconds
3. **Stuck bolt**: Apply graphite lubricant every 3 months
