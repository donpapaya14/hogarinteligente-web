## Introduction to UV Phone Sanitizers
A **UV phone sanitizer with charging** is an essential tool for maintaining a **germ-free phone**. According to a [National Institutes of Health (NIH)](https://www.nih.gov) study on UV-C disinfection (2020), UV-C light is effective against **bacteria and viruses**, including common pathogens found on phones like **E. coli** and **Staphylococcus**.

## How UV-C Light Kills Germs on Phones
The science behind UV-C light's effectiveness lies in its ability to damage the DNA of microorganisms, ultimately killing them. This process is crucial for keeping your phone clean, as it can harbor **more bacteria than a toilet seat**.

## Why Your Phone is Dirtier Than a Toilet Seat
A [University of Arizona](https://www.arizona.edu) microbiome study (2022) found that phones have an average of **9,000 colony-forming units (CFU) per square centimeter**, compared to **1,000 CFU** on a toilet seat. This highlights the importance of regularly sanitizing your phone.

## Top Features to Look for in a UV Sanitizer
When choosing a **UV phone sanitizer box**, consider the following key specs:
* **UV wavelength**: 260–280nm
* **Sanitization time**: 5-10 minutes
* **Charging wattage**: 5V, 2A
* **Compatibility**: Check if it fits your phone size and case
* **Safety certifications**: Look for FDA clearance and other relevant certifications
The [FDA](https://www.fda.gov) guidelines for UV disinfection devices provide more information on these features.

## Step-by-Step: How to Use It Correctly
To use a **UV phone sanitizer** correctly:
1. Place your phone in the sanitizer, ensuring it is centered and the UV-C light can reach all areas.
2. Set the sanitization time according to the manufacturer's instructions (usually 5-10 minutes).
3. Let the cycle complete, then remove your phone and wipe the interior of the sanitizer with a soft cloth.
[PhoneSoap](https://www.phonesoap.com) provides detailed instructions for their products.
