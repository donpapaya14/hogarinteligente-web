## Choosing the Right Size and Material for Your Storage Bed
When selecting a storage bed frame, it's essential to consider factors such as room dimensions, personal style, and budget. According to [This Old House](https://www.thisoldhouse.com), the average bedroom size is around **120 square feet**. To choose the right size, consider the following steps:

By following these steps, you can find the perfect storage bed frame that fits your needs and complements your bedroom decor.

## Optimizing Your Storage Bed's Drawers for Maximum Storage
To maximize storage capacity in your storage bed's drawers, consider the following tips:
* Use dividers to separate items within the drawers.
* Install shelves or baskets to store items such as linens or out-of-season clothing.
* Utilize the space under the bed by using storage bins or drawers.
According to [Real Simple](https://www.realsimple.com), these tips can help increase storage capacity by up to **30%**.
