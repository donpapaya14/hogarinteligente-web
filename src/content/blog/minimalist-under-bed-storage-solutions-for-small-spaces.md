On average, **40%** of the space beneath beds remains unused, which can be optimized with the right storage solutions.

The average person has about **10 square feet** of unused space under their bed, as reported by the [National Association of Professional Organizers](https://www.napo.net/), 2022. ## Key Features of a Minimalist Under-Bed Organizer
When choosing a **minimalist under-bed organizer**, consider the following must-have features:
* Low-profile design

* Easy-glide wheels

## What to Store (and What to Avoid)
When using **minimalist under-bed storage**, it's essential to store the right items. Ideal items include:
1. Seasonal clothing
2. Extra linens
Avoid storing heavy items, as they can cause the bed to sag, and moisture-sensitive materials, as they may be damaged due to lack of ventilation. According to [The Home Edit Method](https://www.thehomeedit.com/), 2021, storing out-of-season clothes can help reduce clutter and make the most of under-bed space.

## Pro Tip: Labeling for Effortless Access
To track stored items without opening boxes, use **color-coded labels** or **QR codes** via apps like [Evernote](https://evernote.com/). As suggested by [Marie Kondo’s Spark Joy](https://www.mariekondo.com/), 2020, labeling storage boxes can help maintain a clutter-free space and make it easier to find what you need.

## Maintenance: Keeping Dust and Pests Out
To keep dust and pests out of under-bed storage, use simple hacks like:
1. Silica gel packs for moisture control
2. Cedar blocks for moth prevention
3. Vacuum-sealing infrequently used items
According to the [EPA Pest Prevention Guidelines](https://www.epa.gov/), 2023, regular cleaning and maintenance can help prevent pest infestations and keep storage areas clean.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best material for under-bed storage?
The best material for under-bed storage is **breathable fabric**, such as canvas, which allows for airflow and helps prevent moisture buildup. 
### How often should I clean under-bed storage?
It's recommended to clean under-bed storage **every 3-6 months** to prevent dust and pest accumulation. 
### Can I store heavy items under my bed?
No, it's not recommended to store heavy items under your bed, as they can cause the bed to sag and potentially damage the frame.

The benefits of using under-bed storage include **increased storage space**, **reduced clutter**, and **easier access** to stored items.
