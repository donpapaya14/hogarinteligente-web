## Measurements most people forget

Before choosing the best under-sink organizer for your small kitchen, it's essential to measure your space accurately. According to the National Kitchen & Bath Association (NKBA) 2023 guidelines, there are three critical dimensions to check:

### Pipe clearance
Measure the distance between the pipes under your sink to ensure the organizer fits comfortably. A general rule of thumb is to leave at least 1 inch (2.5 cm) of clearance on each side.

### Cabinet depth
Check the depth of your cabinet to determine the maximum length of the organizer you can install. Most under-sink organizers are designed to fit standard cabinet depths of 24 inches (61 cm).

### Door swing
Consider the direction your cabinet door swings to avoid any obstructions. If your door swings in, you may need to choose an organizer with a narrower profile.

## Danger zone: What NOT to store here

Certain chemicals and foods degrade in humidity, making them unsuitable for storage under your sink. According to the USDA Food Safety Guidelines 2022, it's essential to store the following items in a well-ventilated area:

> Relacionado: [Pantry Organization to Reduce Food Waste](/blog/pantry-organization-to-reduce-food-waste)

### Chemicals
* Cleaning products with high VOCs (volatile organic compounds)
* Paints and solvents
* Pesticides and insecticides

### Foods
* Fruits and vegetables with high water content (e.g., strawberries, watermelon)
* Dairy products (e.g., milk, eggs)
* Meat and poultry

## Hack: Tension rod + S-hooks

Create vertical storage for spray bottles using a tension rod and S-hooks. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Choose a tension rod that fits your cabinet depth (usually 24 inches or 61 cm).
2. Install the rod in the cabinet, leaving about 1 inch (2.5 cm) of clearance on each side.
3. Attach S-hooks to the rod, spacing them evenly apart (about 2-3 inches or 5-7.5 cm).
4. Hang your spray bottles from the S-hooks, ensuring they're secure and won't fall.
