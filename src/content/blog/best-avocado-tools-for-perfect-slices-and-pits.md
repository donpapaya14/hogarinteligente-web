## Pro Trick: How to Pit an Avocado Without a Tool
If you don't have an avocado tool, you can still pit an avocado safely using a knife. The **lever method** involves inserting the tip of a knife into the pit and twisting it gently to release the pit. According to the [National Culinary Institute](https://www.nationalculinaryinstitute.com) safety guidelines 2021, this method is a safe and effective way to pit an avocado.

## Best Tool for Ripe vs. Unripe Avocados
The best tool for ripe vs. unripe avocados depends on the **blade thickness and serration**. A **thicker blade** with **deep serrations** is best for ripe avocados, while a **thinner blade** with **shallow serrations** is best for unripe avocados. According to the [University of California Agriculture Dept.](https://www.ucanr.edu) 2020, the **stem nub** can be used to determine the ripeness of an avocado.
