## Resolution and Field of View

Choosing the right resolution and field of view depends on your surveillance needs. According to the [Security Industry Association 2020](https://www.securityindustry.org):
- **1080p**: Minimum for clear facial recognition (30ft range).
- **2K/4K**: Ideal for large areas (e.g., driveways) but requires more bandwidth.
- **Field of view**: Narrow (90°) for focused areas like doors; wide (130°+) for yards.

**Pro tip**: For a 2-car driveway, a 130° camera at 1080p captures license plates clearly within 20ft.

## Weather Resistance and Durability

Outdoor security camera systems need robust weatherproofing. The [International Electrotechnical Commission 2019](https://www.iec.ch) standards define:
- **IP65**: Dust-tight and withstands water jets (e.g., heavy rain).
- **IP67**: Submersible in 1m water for 30 minutes (flood-prone areas).

**Durability test**: The Nest Cam IQ Outdoor survived -40°F to 113°F in independent tests (Wirecutter, 2021).

## Night Vision and Motion Detection

Modern night vision cameras use:
- **Infrared (IR)**: Up to 30ft range (monochrome).
- **Starlight sensors**: Color footage in low light (e.g., Arlo Pro 3).

The [National Institute of Standards and Technology 2018](https://www.nist.gov) recommends:
1. Set motion zones to avoid false alerts (e.g., trees).
2. Adjust sensitivity to detect humans (>50lbs) only.
3. Schedule recordings during high-risk hours (10 PM–4 AM).

## Installation and Maintenance Tips

Follow this [DIY Network 2021](https://www.diynetwork.com) guide:
1. **Placement**: 7–9ft high, angled downward (prevents tampering).
2. **Mounting**: Use stainless steel screws for brick walls.
3. **Updates**: Enable auto-updates for firmware (e.g., Ring updates weekly).
4. **Lens care**: Clean monthly with microfiber cloth (prevents blurring).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What’s the best wireless outdoor security camera for cold climates?
The **Arlo Pro 3** operates at -4°F to 113°F (Arlo Labs, 2021). Ensure it’s IP65-rated and uses lithium batteries (not alkaline).

### How far can night vision cameras see?
Most IR cameras cover 30ft, but starlight sensors (e.g., Nest Cam IQ) extend to 50ft with ambient light (NIST, 2018).

### Do outdoor cameras work without Wi-Fi?
Only local storage models (e.g., Reolink Argus 3 with SD card) work offline. Cloud-based cams require Wi-Fi.

### Can weatherproof security cameras withstand hurricanes?
IP67 cameras resist wind-driven rain, but mount them under eaves for added protection (FEMA, 2020).

### How often should I replace batteries?
Lithium batteries last 6–12 months. Solar-powered cams (e.g., Reolink Argus 3) eliminate replacements.
