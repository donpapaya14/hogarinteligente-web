## Introduction to Pantry Organization
To reduce food waste, understanding **pantry organization** is key. According to the [USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/), **40% of food** produced in the United States goes uneaten. Implementing effective **pantry organization ideas** can significantly decrease this statistic.

## Understanding Food Expiration Dates
Decoding expiration dates is crucial for **reducing food waste at home**. The [USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (2022)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/) explains that 'Sell By', 'Use By', and 'Best By' labels are often misunderstood. 
| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Sell By | The last date stores can sell the product |
| Use By | The last date for peak quality |
| Best By | Similar to 'Use By', indicates freshness |

## First-In-First-Out (FIFO) Storage
The FIFO method is essential for reducing food waste. The [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health (2019)](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/) recommends labeling and date tracking to ensure older items are consumed before they expire. 
1. Label each item with the date it was opened or stored.
2. Place newer items behind older ones to maintain the FIFO order.
3. Regularly check expiration dates and consume or freeze items before they expire.
