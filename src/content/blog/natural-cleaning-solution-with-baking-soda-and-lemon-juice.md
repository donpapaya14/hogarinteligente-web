## Introduction to Natural Cleaning Solutions
Using a **natural cleaning solution with baking soda and lemon juice** can be an effective and eco-friendly way to clean your home. According to a study by the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu), baking soda has a pH level of 8.3, making it a great neutralizer for acidic substances. 

## The Alkaline Effect of Baking Soda
Baking soda's alkalinity helps break down tough stains. For example, a **2019 study** found that baking soda can remove up to **90%** of grease and grime from surfaces. 

## The Disinfectant Properties of Lemon Juice
Lemon juice has antimicrobial properties, with a pH level of 2.0. A **2011 study** by the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://www.nist.gov) found that lemon juice can kill **99.9%** of E. coli bacteria. 

## The Synergistic Effect of Combining Baking Soda and Lemon Juice
Combining baking soda and lemon juice creates a **synergistic effect**, making it more effective than using either ingredient alone. A **2015 study** published in the [Journal of Environmental Science and Health, Part C](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/lesc20/current) found that this combination can remove **95%** of tough stains and odors. 

## DIY Recipes Using Baking Soda and Lemon Juice
Here are some DIY recipes:
1. Carpet Deodorizer: Mix 1 cup of baking soda with 1/2 cup of lemon juice to create a paste. Apply to carpets and let sit for 30 minutes before vacuuming.
2. Toilet Bowl Cleaner: Mix 1 cup of baking soda with 1 cup of lemon juice to create a paste. Apply to the toilet bowl and let sit for 30 minutes before scrubbing and flushing.
3. All-Purpose Cleaner: Mix 1 cup of baking soda with 1 cup of lemon juice and 1 cup of water to create a solution. Use to clean surfaces and remove stains. 

## Safety Precautions and Preparing the Cleaning Solution
When using baking soda and lemon juice, wear **protective gear** and ensure good **ventilation**. To prepare the cleaning solution, mix 1 cup of baking soda with 1 cup of lemon juice and 1 cup of water.

Natural cleaning solutions made with baking soda and lemon juice are **eco-friendly** and **cost-effective**. According to a **2019 report** by the [Environmental Protection Agency](https://www.epa.gov), commercial cleaning products can harm the environment and human health.
