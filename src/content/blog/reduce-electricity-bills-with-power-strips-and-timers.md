## Calculating Potential Energy Savings
To calculate potential energy savings using power strips and timers, follow these steps:

1.  **Identify standby devices**: Make a list of all devices in your household that are left in standby mode.
2.  **Measure standby power consumption**: Use a power meter to measure the standby power consumption of each device.
3.  **Calculate energy savings**: Use the following formula to calculate energy savings: Energy Savings = (Standby Power Consumption x Number of Devices x Hours of Operation) / 1000

For example, if you have 5 devices with an average standby power consumption of 5 watts, and you use them for 8 hours a day, your energy savings would be:

Energy Savings = (5 watts x 5 devices x 8 hours) / 1000 = 0.2 kWh
