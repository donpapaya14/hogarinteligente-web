## Section 1: Understanding Solar Panel Installation Costs
The average cost of **solar panel installation** is around $2.94 per watt, according to the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL, 2022). This translates to a total cost of $17,640 for a 6-kilowatt system. The cost can be broken down into equipment (45%), labor (30%), and permits (10%).

## Section 2: Calculating Solar Panel Electricity Savings
To calculate electricity savings with solar panels, you need to consider factors like roof size, panel efficiency, and local energy rates. For example, a homeowner in California with a 2,000 square foot roof and 20 panels can save around $1,200 per year on their energy bill, according to the U.S. Department of Energy (2022).

## Section 3: Realistic Payback Period for Solar Panels
The average payback period for solar panel installations in different regions is around 5-7 years, according to the Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA, 2022). This means that homeowners can expect to break even on their investment in 5-7 years, depending on the location and system size.

## Section 4: Factors Affecting Solar Panel Costs and Savings
Factors like roof orientation, shading, and local incentives can impact solar panel installation costs and electricity savings. For example, a south-facing roof can increase energy production by up to 20%, according to the International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA, 2020). Homeowners can use online tools to assess their roof's suitability for solar panels and estimate potential savings.

## Section 5: Incentives and Tax Credits for Solar Panel Installation
The federal Solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC) allows homeowners to claim a tax credit of up to 26% of the total system cost, according to the U.S. Department of the Treasury (2022). Additionally, some states offer their own incentives and rebates for solar panel installations. Homeowners can use online resources to find out what incentives are available in their area.
