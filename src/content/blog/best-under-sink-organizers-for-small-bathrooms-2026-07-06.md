## Introduction to Under-Sink Organizers for Small Bathrooms
The best under-sink organizers for small bathrooms are designed to maximize storage while considering the unique challenges of small spaces, such as depth measurements and plumbing configurations. According to the [National Kitchen & Bath Association](https://www.nkba.org/) 2022 guidelines, **18" depth** is often required for most pedestal sinks, making standard organizers impractical.

## Why Standard Organizers Fail in Small Bathrooms
Standard organizers fail in small bathrooms due to their inability to accommodate the specific clearance requirements, such as the **need for 18" depth** for most pedestal sinks, as stated in the [National Kitchen & Bath Association](https://www.nkba.org/) 2022 guidelines. This highlights the necessity for specialized designs that cater to these unique spatial constraints.

## 3 Must-Have Features for Tight Spaces
For effective under-sink organization in small bathrooms, consider the following must-have features:
1. **Adjustable tiered shelves** for vertical storage, allowing for customization to fit various bathroom configurations.
2. **Slim-profile materials**; for instance, materials with a thickness of **less than 2mm** can provide significant space savings compared to thicker alternatives.
3. **Cut-out backs** for pipe clearance, ensuring that the organizer does not obstruct plumbing, as recommended by [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/) 2023 small space solutions test.

Before purchasing an under-sink organizer, measure the following critical dimensions:
1. **Sink bowl protrusion depth**
2. **Cold water valve position**
3. **Bottom cabinet door hinge clearance**
