A study by the [National Kitchen and Bath Association](https://www.nkba.org) (2020) found that the average kitchen appliance lasts for **10-15 years**, but this lifespan can be significantly reduced by water damage.

## The Most Common Culprit: Detergent Residue
Detergent residue is a common culprit in reducing appliance lifespan, as it can cause **corrosion** and **clogging**. To prevent this, it is essential to properly clean and dry appliances after washing dishes. The [Appliance Repair Council](https://www.appliancerepaircouncil.org) (2015) recommends cleaning appliances with a **soft cloth** and **mild detergent**.
