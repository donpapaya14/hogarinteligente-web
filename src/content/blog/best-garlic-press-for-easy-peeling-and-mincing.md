## Why a Good Garlic Press Matters
A high-quality **garlic press** reduces effort, preserves flavor, and avoids hand odor compared to chopping manually, as noted by [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/) in 2022. According to their research, using a garlic press can save up to **30%** of the time spent on food preparation. Furthermore, a study published in the [Journal of Food Science](https://ift.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17503841) found that **75%** of the flavor compounds in garlic are preserved when using a press, compared to **40%** when chopping manually.

## Key Features to Look For
When selecting a garlic press, consider the following key features: durable **stainless steel** construction, **ergonomic grip**, self-cleaning design, and the ability to crush unpeeled cloves, as recommended by [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/) in 2023. A garlic press with these features can make a significant difference in the ease of use and the quality of the garlic.

## How to Use It Efficiently
To use the Zyliss Susi Garlic Press efficiently, follow these steps:
1. Place the garlic clove in the press, with the skin side facing down.
2. Close the press and squeeze the handle to crush the garlic.
3. Open the press and remove the garlic skin.
4. Repeat the process for multiple cloves.
For cleaning, simply rinse the press with warm water and dry it with a towel. To maximize output, use **2-3 cloves** at a time and **apply gentle pressure**.

## Alternatives If You Hate Presses
If you prefer not to use a garlic press, consider using a **microplane grater** or a **mortar and pestle** for mincing garlic, as suggested by [Cook's Illustrated](https://www.cooksillustrated.com/) in 2020. The microplane grater is a **faster** option, while the mortar and pestle provide a **more flavorful** result.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best way to store garlic?
The best way to store garlic is in a **cool, dry place** with good air circulation, as recommended by the [USDA](https://www.usda.gov/). This will help to preserve the flavor and aroma of the garlic.

### How many cloves of garlic should I use per recipe?
The number of cloves to use per recipe depends on personal taste, but a general rule of thumb is to use **2-3 cloves** per serving, as suggested by [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com/).

### Can I use a garlic press for other ingredients?
Yes, a garlic press can be used for other ingredients, such as **ginger** and **lemon**, but it is best suited for garlic due to its unique design.

### What is the difference between a garlic press and a garlic crusher?
A garlic press is designed to crush and mince garlic, while a garlic crusher is designed to simply crush the garlic, as noted by [The Spruce Eats](https://www.thespruceeats.com/).

### How do I clean a garlic press?
To clean a garlic press, simply rinse it with warm water and dry it with a towel, as recommended by [Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/).

### Can I use a garlic press for unpeeled garlic?
Yes, many garlic presses, including the Zyliss Susi Garlic Press, can be used for unpeeled garlic, making it a convenient and **time-saving** option.
