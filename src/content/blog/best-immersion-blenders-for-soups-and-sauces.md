## Introduction to Immersion Blenders
When it comes to preparing soups and sauces, **best immersion blenders for soups** can be a game-changer. According to a study by [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org), the right immersion blender can make a significant difference in the quality of your dishes.

## Why Power (Watts) Doesn’t Tell the Whole Story
The power of an immersion blender, measured in watts, is often considered a key factor in its performance. However, **motor efficiency**, **blade design**, and **weight distribution** are equally important. As noted in [Consumer Reports 2023 Blender Lab Tests](https://www.consumerreports.org), top models have RPM ranges of 10,000 to 20,000.

* Top models: 10,000 - 20,000 RPM
* Mid-range models: 5,000 - 15,000 RPM
* Budget models: 3,000 - 10,000 RPM

## The Hidden Feature That Prevents Splattering
A key feature to look for in an immersion blender is an **anti-splash design**. This can include **vortex blades** or **guard shapes** that help to prevent splattering. As shown in a slow-motion video by [America's Test Kitchen 2022 Immersion Blender Review](https://www.americastestkitchen.com), these designs can make a significant difference.

## Stainless Steel vs. Nylon Blades: Which Lasts Longer?
When it comes to the **blades** of an immersion blender, **stainless steel** and **nylon** are two common materials. According to a study by [Cook's Illustrated Durability Study 2021](https://www.cooksillustrated.com), stainless steel blades can last up to 50% longer than nylon blades after 100 hours of use.

### How do I prevent splattering when using an immersion blender?
To prevent splattering, use an immersion blender with an anti-splash design, and make sure to blend slowly and carefully. As noted in [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com), a slow-motion video can help to illustrate the importance of this feature.
### What is the difference between stainless steel and nylon blades?
Stainless steel blades are more durable and can last up to 50% longer than nylon blades, according to [Cook's Illustrated](https://www.cooksillustrated.com).
### Can I use an immersion blender for other tasks besides soups and sauces?
Yes, immersion blenders can be used for a variety of tasks, including making hummus, guacamole, and even frozen drinks. As noted in [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com), they are a versatile tool to have in the kitchen.
### How do I clean an immersion blender?
To clean an immersion blender, simply wash the blades and shaft with soap and water, and dry thoroughly. According to [Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com), this can help to prevent the growth of bacteria and other microorganisms.
