## Why Avocados Brown So Fast
Avocados contain an enzyme called polyphenol oxidase, which reacts with oxygen in the air to turn the avocado's phenolic compounds into brown pigments. The [USDA Agricultural Research Service](https://www.ars.usda.gov) found that this process can be slowed down, but not completely stopped, by using lemon juice or other acidic substances. However, the most effective way to prevent browning is to remove oxygen from the environment, which is where the **best avocado saver for freshness** comes in.
