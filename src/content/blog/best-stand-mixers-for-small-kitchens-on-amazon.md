## Stand Mixer Features to Consider for Small Kitchens
When choosing a stand mixer for a small kitchen, consider the following features:
1. **Bowl size**: A smaller bowl size (around 3-4 quarts) is ideal for small kitchens.
2. **Speed settings**: Look for a mixer with multiple speed settings (around 10-12) for versatility.
3. **Attachments**: Consider a mixer with attachments like a **whisk**, **dough hook**, and **scraping attachment** for various tasks.
