## Effective Labeling and Inventory Systems
Labeling and inventory systems are crucial for maintaining a well-organized kitchen pantry. According to **Kitchen Organization Magazine's Guide to Labeling and Inventory Systems (2020)**, a good labeling system can reduce food waste by up to 30%. To create a labeling system, use a label maker and categorize your food items into groups such as grains, canned goods, and baking supplies. You can also use apps like **Out of Milk** to manage your pantry inventory.
