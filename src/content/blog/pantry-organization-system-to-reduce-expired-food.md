## Introduction to Pantry Organization
To reduce expired food and prevent **food waste**, implementing a **pantry organization system** is crucial. According to the [USDA](https://www.usda.gov), about 30-40% of the food supply in the United States goes uneaten. A well-organized pantry helps in **food waste prevention** by ensuring that older products are consumed before they expire.

## First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Method Explained
The **FIFO method** is a simple yet effective way to ensure that older products are used before they expire. This can be achieved by labeling products with their expiration dates and placing them in a way that the oldest items are at the front. The [USDA FoodKeeper App](https://www.usda.gov/topics/food-safety) (2023) provides guidance on how to store different types of food to maximize their shelf life.

1. Label each product with its expiration date.
2. Place the oldest items at the front of the shelf.
3. Regularly check the expiration dates of the products.

## Clear Container Strategy for Visibility
Using **transparent containers** can help reduce forgotten food by making it visible. According to a study published in the [Journal of Consumer Psychology](https://www.journalofconsumerpsychology.org) (2019), consumers are more likely to use products that are visible. Choosing the right size of containers is also important to prevent overbuying and waste.

| --- | --- |
| Small | Spices, herbs |
| Medium | Grains, pasta |

## Zone-Based Categorization for Faster Checks
Dividing the pantry into **zones** can make it easier to check the expiration dates of the products. The [National Association of Professional Organizers](https://www.napo.net) (2022) suggests categorizing products into zones such as baking, snacks, and canned goods. A printable zone map template can be used to create a customized plan.

## Monthly 'Pantry Audit' Routine
Conducting a monthly **pantry audit** can help identify soon-to-expire items and prevent waste. The [EPA Food Recovery Hierarchy](https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food) (2021) provides a framework for reducing food waste. Here is a 5-minute checklist to identify soon-to-expire items:
1. Check the expiration dates of the products.
2. Identify soon-to-expire items.
3. Plan meals using these items.
