## Introduction to Eufy RoboVac G30 and L70
The **Eufy RoboVac G30** and **Eufy RoboVac L70** are two popular smart vacuum models designed for efficient cleaning. According to the [Eufy official website](https://www.eufylife.com), both models feature advanced navigation systems, long-lasting batteries, and powerful suction capabilities.

The **Eufy RoboVac G30** has a suction power of **2000Pa**, a battery life of up to **100 minutes**, and a slim design for easy navigation under furniture. In contrast, the **Eufy RoboVac L70** boasts a **2200Pa** suction power, a **120-minute** battery life, and an advanced laser navigation system.
