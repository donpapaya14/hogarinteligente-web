## Section 1: Understanding Mold and Mildew Growth
Mold and mildew growth can be caused by excess moisture, poor ventilation, and lack of cleaning. The [Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)](https://www.epa.gov) recommends regular cleaning and disinfection to prevent mold and mildew growth. Signs of infestation include black spots, musty odors, and water stains.

## Section 6: Preventing Future Mold and Mildew Growth
To prevent future mold and mildew growth, it's essential to:
1. **Improve ventilation**: ensure good airflow in bathrooms to prevent moisture buildup.
2. **Clean regularly**: clean bathroom surfaces regularly to prevent mold and mildew growth.
3. **Fix leaks**: fix any leaks or water damage to prevent moisture buildup.
According to the [EPA](https://www.epa.gov), these strategies can help prevent mold and mildew growth and reduce the risk of health issues.
