## Introduction to the Instant Pot Duo Plus
The **Instant Pot Duo Plus** is a 7-in-1 **pressure cooker** and **multi cooker** that combines 15 built-in programs, including slow cooking, rice cooking, and yogurt making. According to the [Instant Pot Official Website](https://www.instantpot.com), it features **smart programming** with automatic adjustments for altitude and temperature, making it versatile for various cooking needs. Its 6-quart capacity (also available in 3- and 8-quart sizes) suits families of 4–6, and it reduces cooking time by up to 70% compared to conventional methods.

Key features:
- **Pressure cooking**: Reaches 15 PSI for faster cooking (e.g., 8-minute hard-boiled eggs).
- **Slow cooking**: Low (190°F) and high (200°F) settings for 4–10 hour recipes.
- **Yogurt mode**: Maintains 110°F for fermentation (per USDA food safety guidelines).

## Cleaning and Maintenance Tips
**Instant Pot Customer Support** recommends:
1. **Daily cleaning**: Remove the sealing ring and wash with warm soapy water (prevents odor retention).
2. **Monthly descaling**: Use 1 cup white vinegar + 2 cups water in “Steam” mode for 5 minutes.
3. **Troubleshooting**: Error code “C6” indicates overheating—unplug for 30 minutes before restarting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does the Instant Pot Duo Plus take to pressurize?
The Duo Plus takes **8–15 minutes** to reach pressure, depending on food volume. A **University of Nebraska study (2021)** found electric pressure cookers average 10 minutes vs. 5 for stovetop models.

### Can you slow cook and pressure cook simultaneously?
No. The Duo Plus operates **one function at a time**, though its “Keep Warm” mode activates automatically after pressure cooking (per Instant Pot’s manual).

### Is the Instant Pot Duo Plus dishwasher safe?
Only the **inner pot, lid ring, and accessories** are dishwasher-safe. The base unit must be wiped clean to avoid electrical damage.

### What’s the difference between Duo Plus and Duo Evo Plus?
