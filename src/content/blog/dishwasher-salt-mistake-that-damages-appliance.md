## Why Dishwasher Salt Matters
Dishwasher salt is crucial for maintaining your appliance's lifespan, especially in areas with **hard water**. According to the [Consumer Reports Appliance Longevity Study (2022)](https://www.consumerreports.org/), using dishwasher salt can prevent **limescale buildup** in internal components, such as the heating element and pump. Skipping salt in hard water areas can accelerate wear and reduce the appliance's lifespan.

## The Silent Mistake Most Owners Make
Overfilling the salt compartment can cause clogs and corrosion, while underfilling leaves residue. The ideal fill level is **1/2 to 2/3 full**, as recommended by the [Bosch Dishwasher Maintenance Manual (2023)](https://www.bosch-home.com/). To avoid this mistake, always check the salt level before running a cycle.

## How to Test Your Water Hardness
Testing your water hardness is essential to determine if you need to use dishwasher salt. You can use **free test strips** or check your municipal water reports. According to the [US Geological Survey Water Hardness Map (2021)](https://www.usgs.gov/), water hardness levels above **7 grains per gallon** require salt. Here's a step-by-step guide to test your water hardness:
1. Purchase a water test strip kit or check your municipal water report.
2. Follow the instructions provided with the test strip kit or check the report for the water hardness level.
3. Compare your results to the recommended thresholds.
