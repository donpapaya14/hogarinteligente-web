According to the [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), 2022, microwave ovens account for approximately 3% of total household energy consumption. When used for defrosting, microwaves can consume even more energy due to the longer cooking times required.

## The Common Defrosting Mistake
One of the most common mistakes people make when defrosting in the microwave is **overheating**, which can lead to increased **energy consumption**. As reported by [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org), 2020, overheating can occur when food is not defrosted properly, resulting in a longer cooking time and increased energy consumption.

## Correct Defrosting Techniques
To defrost food correctly in the microwave and minimize **energy consumption**, follow these steps:
1. **Check the food**: Before defrosting, check the food for any visible signs of spoilage.
2. **Use the defrost setting**: Use the defrost setting on your microwave to defrost food safely and efficiently.
3. **Cover the food**: Cover the food with a microwave-safe lid or plastic wrap to prevent splatters and promote even defrosting.
4. **Defrost in intervals**: Defrost food in short intervals, checking and flipping the food as needed.
