## Why Avocados Brown So Fast
Avocados contain an enzyme called polyphenol oxidase, which reacts with oxygen in the air to turn the avocado's phenolic compounds into brown pigments. This reaction can be slowed down by reducing oxygen exposure. A time-lapse of an untreated avocado browning can be seen [here](https://www.youtube.com).
