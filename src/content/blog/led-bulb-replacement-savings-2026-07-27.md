## Introduction to LED Bulbs
Replacing traditional incandescent bulbs with **LED bulbs** can significantly reduce energy consumption. According to the [U.S. Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), LED bulbs consume **up to 90% less power** than traditional incandescent bulbs. For example, a traditional 60-watt incandescent bulb can be replaced with a 6-watt LED bulb, resulting in **84% less energy consumption**.

## Calculating Potential Savings
To calculate potential annual savings, follow these steps:
1. Determine the number of traditional bulbs in your home.
2. Calculate the total wattage of these bulbs.
3. Replace these bulbs with LED bulbs and calculate the new total wattage.
4. Multiply the difference in wattage by the number of hours the bulbs are used per year.
5. Multiply the result by your electricity rate (in dollars per kilowatt-hour).
For example, if you replace 10 traditional 60-watt bulbs with 6-watt LED bulbs, and these bulbs are used for 8 hours per day, you can save **$120 per year** on your electricity bill, based on an average US household energy consumption of **900 kilowatt-hours per month**, according to the [Energy Information Administration](https://www.eia.gov).

## Choosing the Right LED Bulbs
When selecting LED bulbs, consider the following factors:
* **Lumens**: Measure of brightness. For example, a 60-watt incandescent bulb is equivalent to an **800-lumen LED bulb**.
* **Color temperature**: Measure of warmth or coolness of the light. For example, **warm white** (2700K-3000K) is suitable for living rooms, while **cool white** (3500K-5000K) is suitable for kitchens.
* **Dimmability**: Ability to adjust brightness. Look for bulbs with **dimmable** or **smart** features.

## Installation and Maintenance Tips
When replacing traditional bulbs with LED bulbs, follow these safety tips:
1. Turn off the power to the lighting fixture before replacing the bulb.
2. Handle the new LED bulb carefully to avoid damaging it.
3. Dispose of the old bulb properly, according to the [Environmental Protection Agency](https://www.epa.gov) guidelines.
To maintain the lifespan of your LED bulbs, avoid exposing them to extreme temperatures and humidity.
