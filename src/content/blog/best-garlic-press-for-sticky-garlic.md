## Pro Tip: How to Prep Garlic to Minimize Sticking
To minimize sticking, it's essential to prep the garlic correctly. According to a study by the [Journal of Food Science](https://www.ift.org) in 2021, crushing **unpeeled cloves** can reduce stickiness. Here's a step-by-step guide:
1. Peel the garlic cloves and place them on a flat surface.
2. Use a **heavy object** to crush the cloves.
3. Remove the peel and use the crushed garlic as desired.

## Maintenance Tricks to Keep Your Press Sticky-Free
To keep your garlic press sticky-free, it's essential to maintain it correctly. According to [Cook's Illustrated](https://www.cookillustrated.com) in 2022, **immediate rinse** is the best way to clean a garlic press. Here are some maintenance tricks to keep your press sticky-free:
1. Rinse the press immediately after use.
2. Soak the press in **warm water** for 10 minutes.
3. Avoid using a **dishwasher**, as it can damage the press.
