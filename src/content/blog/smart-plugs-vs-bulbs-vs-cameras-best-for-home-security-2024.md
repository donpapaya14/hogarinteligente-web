## Security Features: Which Deters Intruders Best?
**Cameras** offer surveillance, but **smart plugs** and **bulbs** can simulate occupancy. According to a study by the [University of North Carolina Dept. of Criminology (2022)](https://www.unc.edu), burglary rates can be reduced by up to **60%** with the use of security cameras. However, **smart plugs** and **bulbs** can also be effective in deterring intruders. Here are some real-world burglary deterrence stats:
* **63%** of burglars said they would avoid a home with a security system (Source: [National Council for Home Safety and Security](https://www.nchss.org))
* **57%** of burglars said they would choose a different target if they saw a security camera (Source: [Security Magazine](https://www.securitymagazine.com))

## Setup & Compatibility: Alexa, Google, Apple Home
When it comes to setup and compatibility, **smart plugs** and **bulbs** are generally easier to install than **cameras**. According to the [SmartThings Compatibility Report (2023)](https://www.smartthings.com), **Kasa Smart Plugs** work seamlessly with **Alexa** and **Google Home**. Here are some common setup pitfalls to avoid:
1. **2.4GHz Wi-Fi requirements**: Make sure your router is set to 2.4GHz for optimal connectivity.
2. **Power cycle**: Restart your devices to ensure proper connectivity.

## Energy Savings: Do Smart Plugs Actually Cut Bills?
**Smart plugs** can help reduce energy consumption by turning off devices when not in use. According to an [Energy Star Case Study (2023)](https://www.energystar.gov), **smart plugs** can save up to **$100** per year on energy bills. Here are some measured kWh savings from **smart plugs**:
* **10%** reduction in energy consumption with **smart plugs** (Source: [Energy Star](https://www.energystar.gov))
* **20%** reduction in energy consumption with **smart bulbs** (Source: [Energy.gov](https://www.energy.gov))

## Renters vs Homeowners: Best Pick for Your Situation
For renters, **bulbs** are a great option as they do not require any wiring. For homeowners, **cameras** are a better choice as they provide surveillance and can be installed permanently. According to the [Apartment Therapy Smart Home Guide (2024)](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com), **landlord-friendly alternatives** include **smart plugs** and **portable cameras**.
