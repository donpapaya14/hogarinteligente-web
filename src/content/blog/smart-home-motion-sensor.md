## Introduction to Motion Sensors

Motion sensor light switches detect movement to automatically control lighting, enhancing **smart home automation** while improving **energy efficient lighting**. According to the [National Institute of Standards and Technology (2022)](https://www.nist.gov), these devices use infrared, ultrasonic, or microwave technology to identify motion within a **5-30 foot range**, reducing energy waste by 30-50% in residential spaces. Wireless motion sensors eliminate manual switching, making them ideal for hallways, garages, and outdoor areas.

## Installation and Setup Guide

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker (verified with a voltage tester).
2. Remove the existing switch and connect wires to the motion sensor light switch per [The Spruce (2022)](https://www.thespruce.com) guidelines: **black (hot), white (neutral), green (ground)**.
3. Mount the sensor **6-8 feet high** for optimal detection.
4. Program settings via app (e.g., **5-30 minute auto-off**).

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **False triggers**: Lower sensitivity or reposition the sensor (Smart Home Forum, 2022).
- **No power**: Check circuit breaker and wiring connections.
- **Delayed response**: Update firmware or switch to a **5GHz Wi-Fi** network.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do motion sensor light switches save energy?
They automatically turn off lights when no motion is detected. The U.S. DOE estimates **30-50% less energy use** compared to manual switches.

### Can motion sensors work through walls?
No. Most infrared sensors require **direct line-of-sight**, though some dual-tech models combine microwave for limited wall penetration.

### What’s the best height to install a motion sensor?
**6-8 feet** is ideal for detecting human movement without pet interference (Consumer Reports, 2023).

### Do motion sensors work in cold weather?
Yes, but extreme cold (<14°F) may reduce battery life. Hardwired models like Lutron avoid this issue.

### How long do motion sensor bulbs last?
LED bulbs in sensors average **25,000 hours** (15+ years at 4 hrs/day) per ENERGY STAR data.
