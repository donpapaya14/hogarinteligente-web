## What is a Microfiber Mop with Built-in Water Tank?
A microfiber mop with a built-in water tank is defined as a mop that uses microfiber strands to pick up dirt and dust, with a water tank attached to the handle for easy cleaning. The benefits of using such a mop include reduced water consumption and improved cleaning efficiency. As stated by [The Spruce (2022)](https://www.thespruce.com), microfiber mops with water tanks are available in different types, including string mops and spin mops.

## How Does a Microfiber Mop with Built-in Water Tank Work?
According to [Consumer Reports (2020)](https://www.consumerreports.org), a microfiber mop with a built-in water tank works by using a step-by-step system to clean floors. The water tank system allows for easy filling and emptying of water, while the microfiber head design picks up dirt and dust effectively. To use it effectively, follow these steps:
1. Fill the water tank with the recommended amount of water.
2. Dip the microfiber head into the water tank to moisten it.
3. Wring out the microfiber head to remove excess water.
4. Mop the floor gently, working in sections.
