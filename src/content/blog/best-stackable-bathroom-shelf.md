## Measuring Your Bathroom Space

Follow these steps to ensure perfect fit:

1. **Measure vertical space**: Allow 12" clearance above shelves for accessibility ([The Spruce, 2022](https://www.thespruce.com))
2. **Check depth**: Standard shelves are 8-12" deep - measure from wall to nearest obstruction
3. **Account for plumbing**: Leave 4" clearance around pipes
4. **Test weight capacity**: Most shelves hold 15-30 lbs per tier

For corner units, use the 3-4-5 triangle method to verify right angles.

## Maintenance and Cleaning

[Good Housekeeping (2022)](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com) recommends:

1. **Weekly**: Wipe with vinegar solution (1:3 vinegar:water ratio)
2. **Monthly**: Check for rust (treat immediately with naval jelly)
3. **Quarterly**: Re-tighten all fasteners
4. **Annually**: Reapply waterproofing spray

For plastic shelves, avoid bleach - it causes 72% faster material degradation ([Material Sciences Journal, 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com)).
