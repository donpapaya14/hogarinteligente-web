## Introduction to Minimalist Garage Organization
A clutter-free garage can increase safety, reduce stress, and improve productivity, as found by the National Association of Professional Organizers (2020). The minimalist organization method involves removing unnecessary items and optimizing storage. According to [National Association of Professional Organizers](https://www.napo.net), 67% of homeowners report feeling more organized after decluttering.

## Assessing Your Garage Storage Needs
To evaluate your garage's storage requirements, follow these steps:
1. Measure the space to determine the maximum storage capacity.
2. Categorize items into groups, such as tools, sports equipment, and seasonal decorations.
3. Prioritize storage needs based on frequency of use and importance. As noted by [The Spruce](https://www.thespruce.com), a well-organized garage can increase a home's value by up to 5%.

## Implementing a Labeling and Signage System
