## Understanding Humidity and Its Effects
Humidity control without a dehumidifier is possible by understanding the impact of humidity on indoor air quality. According to the **ASHRAE Standard 55-2013**, the ideal relative humidity (RH) range for human comfort is between **30-60%**. Exceeding this range can lead to discomfort, mold growth, and other health issues.

## Principles of Evaporative Cooling
Evaporative cooling is a natural process that can be used for humidity control. The **National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) Study, 2019** explains that evaporative cooling systems use the principle of evaporation to cool the air, which can reduce the RH. For example, evaporative cooling systems like **swamp coolers** can be used to cool and dehumidify the air.

## Materials for Natural Dehumidification
Natural dehumidification materials like **silica gel**, **activated charcoal**, and **zeolites** can be used to control humidity. A **University of California, Berkeley, Study, 2015** found that these materials have high adsorption capacities, making them effective for humidity control.

## DIY Dehumidification Methods
Here are some DIY dehumidification methods:
1. Use **desiccant packets** to absorb moisture from the air.
2. Install a **damp-proof membrane** to prevent moisture from entering the room.
3. Create an **evaporative cooling system** using a fan and a wet pad.
