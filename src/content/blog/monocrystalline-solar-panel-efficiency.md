## Introduction to Monocrystalline Solar Panels

Monocrystalline solar panels are the most efficient solar technology available today, with **efficiency rates of 15-22%**, compared to 13-16% for polycrystalline panels [National Renewable Energy Laboratory, 2022]. These panels are made from single-crystal silicon, which allows electrons to move more freely, resulting in higher energy output per square foot. 

Key advantages include:
1. **Space efficiency**: Produces 20% more power in the same space as polycrystalline panels
2. **Longevity**: Typically last 25-40 years with proper maintenance
3. **Performance in low light**: Maintains 85-90% efficiency on cloudy days

The average payback period is **6-12 years** when accounting for the 26% federal tax credit [International Renewable Energy Agency, 2022]. Key factors:

3. **Energy inflation**: Every 3% rate increase shortens payback by 6 months

## Maintenance and Durability of Monocrystalline Solar Panels

Monocrystalline panels require minimal maintenance but follow these steps for peak performance:
1. **Quarterly cleaning**: Use soft brush and water (no chemicals)
2. **Annual inspection**: Check for rodent damage or loose wiring
3. **Snow removal**: Use roof rake for accumulations over 2 inches
