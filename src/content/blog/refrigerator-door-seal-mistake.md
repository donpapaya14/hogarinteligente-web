## Introduction to Refrigerator Door Seals
A well-maintained **refrigerator door seal** is crucial for **energy saving** and **fridge energy efficiency**. According to the [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov), 2020, a proper door seal can reduce energy consumption by **10-20%**. 

## Common Mistakes with Refrigerator Door Seals
Common mistakes include incorrect installation, dirty seals, and worn-out seals. A study by [LG Electronics](https://www.lg.com), 2019, found that **70%** of refrigerator owners are unaware of the importance of door seal maintenance.

## Checking Your Refrigerator Door Seal
To inspect your fridge door seal, follow these steps:
1. **Visually inspect** the seal for signs of wear or damage.
2. **Check the seal's flexibility** by gently stretching it.
3. **Test the seal's tightness** by closing the door on a dollar bill.

## Replacing Your Refrigerator Door Seal
Replacing a worn-out or damaged fridge door seal is a straightforward process:
1. **Purchase a replacement seal**, such as the [GE WD35X210 36-Inch Refrigerator Door Seal](https://www.amazon.com/GE-WD35X210-36-Inch-Refrigerator-Door-Seal).
2. **Remove the old seal** by gently pulling it away from the door.
3. **Install the new seal** by aligning it with the door and pressing it into place.

## Tips for Maintaining Your Refrigerator Door Seal
To keep your fridge door seal clean and functional:
1. **Clean the seal regularly** with a mixture of soap and water.
2. **Check the seal's condition** every 6 months.
3. **Replace the seal** every 5-7 years or as needed.
