A well-organized closet can have a significant impact on both physical and mental health. According to the [National Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org), a cluttered closet can lead to increased stress levels, which can negatively affect sleep quality. In fact, a study by the National Sleep Foundation (2020) found that **75% of people** who made their bed every morning reported getting a better night's sleep.

## Maximizing Vertical Space with Double Rods and Shelves
Maximizing vertical space in your closet is crucial for a minimalist closet organization system. According to [Real Simple](https://www.realsimple.com), using double rods and shelves can help increase storage space by **50%**. Here are some steps to follow:
1. Install double rods to double your hanging space.
2. Use stackable shelves to store folded items, such as sweaters and jeans.
3. Consider using a shoe rack or over-the-door storage for shoes and accessories.

## Implementing a Color-Coded Organizational System
A color-coded organizational system can help you quickly find what you need in your closet. According to [Closet Factory](https://www.closetfactory.com), categorizing your clothes by color can help reduce decision fatigue by **30%**. Here's a step-by-step guide to implementing a color-coded system:
1. Sort your clothes into categories (e.g., tops, bottoms, dresses).
2. Assign a color to each category (e.g., blue for tops, green for bottoms).
3. Use colored bins or baskets to store each category.

## Utilizing Baskets and Containers for Accessories and Linens
Baskets and containers can help keep accessories and linens organized and out of sight. According to [The Container Store](https://www.containerstore.com), using baskets and containers can help reduce clutter by **25%**. Here are some recommendations:
* Use a basket to store scarves and hats.
* Use a container to store socks and underwear.
* Consider using a [Seville Classics UltraHD 2-Drawer Storage Cabinet](https://www.amazon.com/Seville-Classics-UltraHD-2-Drawer-Storage-Cabinet) for storing linens and out-of-season clothes.

## Maintaining Your Closet's Organization System
Maintaining your closet's organization system is crucial to keeping your space clutter-free. According to [Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com), setting aside **10 minutes** each week to tidy up your closet can help prevent clutter from building up. Here are some tips:
1. Set a reminder to tidy up your closet each week.
2. Use a vacuum cleaner to dust and clean the floor and shelves.
