## Introduction to Wall Outlet Tamper Resistant Covers
**Wall outlet tamper resistant covers** are an essential component of **child safety** and **electrical safety** in homes. According to the [US Consumer Product Safety Commission (2020)](https://www.cpsc.gov/), electrical shock injuries in households result in approximately **2,400 children** under the age of 10 being treated in U.S. emergency departments each year. These accidents could have been prevented with the use of tamper resistant outlets.

## Why Tamper Resistant Outlets Are Important
The [US Consumer Product Safety Commission (2020)](https://www.cpsc.gov/) reports that **70%** of electrical shock injuries to children under the age of 10 occur when they insert objects into electrical outlets. Examples of such accidents include children inserting keys, pens, or other objects into outlets, resulting in severe injuries or even death. Installing **wall outlet tamper resistant covers** can prevent these types of accidents.

## How Tamper Resistant Covers Work
Tamper resistant covers work by preventing children from inserting objects into outlets. According to the [National Electrical Manufacturers Association (NEMA)](https://www.nema.org/), these covers have a spring-loaded mechanism that only allows plugs to be inserted when both prongs are aligned correctly. This mechanism prevents children from inserting objects into the outlets, thereby reducing the risk of electrical shock.

## Types of Tamper Resistant Covers Available
There are several types of tamper resistant covers available, including:
* Adhesive-backed covers: easy to install, but may not be suitable for all types of outlets
* Screw-in covers: more secure, but may require additional installation time
According to [Amazon product reviews and ratings](https://www.amazon.com/), the pros and cons of each type of cover should be considered when making a purchase.

## Installation and Maintenance Tips
To install tamper resistant covers, follow these steps:
1. Turn off the power to the outlet at the circuit breaker or fuse box.
2. Remove any existing outlet covers.
3. Install the tamper resistant cover according to the manufacturer's instructions.
4. Turn the power back on and test the outlet.
The [US Consumer Product Safety Commission (2020)](https://www.cpsc.gov/) also recommends checking for recalls and replacing worn-out covers regularly.

## Additional Safety Measures for Electrical Safety
In addition to installing tamper resistant covers, other electrical safety measures should be taken, such as:
* Installing **ground fault circuit interrupters (GFCIs)** in areas where water is present, such as kitchens and bathrooms
* Avoiding overloading outlets with too many appliances
The [National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)](https://www.nfpa.org/) recommends taking these measures to reduce the risk of electrical shock and fires.
