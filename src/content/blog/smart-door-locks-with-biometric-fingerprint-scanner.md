## Introduction to Smart Door Locks with Fingerprint Scanner
Smart door locks with fingerprint scanners are revolutionizing home security by providing **keyless entry** and **enhanced security**. According to a [National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)](https://www.nist.gov) 2020 report on biometric authentication, fingerprint scanning is a reliable method for user authentication. 

## How Smart Door Locks with Fingerprint Scanner Work
The technology behind biometric fingerprint scanning involves **optical**, **capacitive**, or **ultrasonic** scanning methods to capture and verify fingerprints. This process integrates with smart home systems through **encryption methods** and **user authentication processes**. A study by the [National Bureau of Standards](https://www.nist.gov) (1985) on the security benefits of biometric authentication found that fingerprint scanning can reduce **unauthorized access** by up to **90%**.

## Benefits of Biometric Fingerprint Scanning for Home Security
Using fingerprint scanning over traditional key-based locks offers several advantages, including:
* **Enhanced security**: Fingerprint scanning provides an additional layer of security, making it more difficult for intruders to gain access.
* **Keyless entry**: Fingerprint scanning eliminates the need for physical keys, reducing the risk of lost or stolen keys.
* **Increased convenience**: Fingerprint scanning allows for quick and easy entry, without the need to carry keys or remember codes.
