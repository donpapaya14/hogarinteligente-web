## Introduction to Minimalist Desk Organization for Small Spaces
Creating a **minimalist desk organization** system for **small spaces** can significantly improve productivity and reduce stress. According to a study by the [National Alliance on Mental Illness](https://www.nami.org), clutter can have a profound impact on mental health, with **67% of people** experiencing increased anxiety due to cluttered workspaces.

## Benefits of Minimalist Desk Organization
A clutter-free workspace can lead to **reduced stress**, **increased focus**, and **improved time management**. A study by the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://www.ucla.edu) found that individuals who worked in organized environments experienced a **31% increase in productivity**. To achieve this, consider implementing the following steps:
1. **Purge unnecessary items**: Remove any items that are no longer needed or used.
2. **Categorize remaining items**: Group similar items together, such as papers, office supplies, and electronics.
3. **Assign a home for each item**: Designate a specific place for each item, making it easier to find and put back.

## Categorizing and Storing Office Supplies
To maintain a clutter-free workspace, follow these steps to categorize and store office supplies:
1. **Label each category**: Use labels to identify each category of items, such as papers, office supplies, and electronics.
2. **Utilize vertical storage**: Use wall-mounted shelves or storage units to maximize storage space.
3. **Implement a 'one touch' rule**: Deal with each piece of paper or document immediately, either by filing it away, throwing it away, or taking action on it.

## Implementing a 'One Touch' Rule
The **'one touch' rule** can significantly reduce clutter and increase productivity. Consider using digital tools, such as [Evernote](https://evernote.com) or [Trello](https://trello.com), to streamline workflow and reduce paper clutter.

## Creating a Maintenance Routine
To maintain a clutter-free workspace, set aside **10-15 minutes each week** to tidy up and review organizational systems. Consider the following tips:
1. **Schedule regular cleaning sessions**: Set a reminder to clean and organize the workspace on a regular basis.
2. **Review organizational systems**: Regularly review and adjust organizational systems to ensure they are working effectively.
