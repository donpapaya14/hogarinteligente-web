## What is a Motion-Sensing Nightlight and How Does it Work?

A **motion-sensing nightlight for stairs** is a smart lighting solution that automatically illuminates when it detects motion, enhancing safety and convenience. These devices use **passive infrared (PIR) sensors** to detect body heat and movement, typically within a range of 10-15 feet. According to a [NIST study (2018)](https://www.nist.gov), PIR sensors can detect motion with 95% accuracy in low-light conditions, making them ideal for nighttime use.

Here’s how it works:
1. **Detection**: The PIR module senses heat signatures from moving objects.
2. **Activation**: The light turns on instantly (usually within 0.5 seconds).
3. **Duration**: Most models stay lit for 15-30 seconds after motion ceases to save energy.

## Installation and Placement Tips

1. **Height**: Install 2-3 feet above stair treads for optimal detection.
2. **Spacing**: Place lights every 4-5 steps for even illumination.
3. **Avoid Obstructions**: Keep sensors clear of curtains or furniture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do motion-sensing nightlight batteries last?
Most **last 6-12 months** with standard alkaline batteries. The LE Smart LED model runs for **8 months** on 3 AA batteries (Amazon testing data).

### Can these lights work outdoors?
Only **weatherproof models** like the Eufy Solar can withstand rain. Standard indoor units may malfunction in humidity.

### What’s the ideal brightness for stair lighting?
**10-30 lumens** is sufficient, per the Illuminating Engineering Society (2021). Brighter lights can cause glare.

### Do they work with carpeted stairs?
Yes, but place sensors **1 foot higher** to detect motion over plush carpets.

### Are there smart home-compatible options?
Philips Hue and LE Smart LED sync with **Alexa and Google Assistant** for voice commands.
