## Tips for Sharpening Knives with Arthritis
Here are some practical tips for sharpening knives with arthritis:
1. **Hold the knife correctly**: Hold the knife at a 20-degree angle, with your thumb on top of the blade and your fingers wrapped around the handle.
2. **Use a sharpening stone**: Use a sharpening stone to hone the edge of the knife, starting with a coarse grit and progressing to a finer grit.
3. **Maintain a sharp edge**: Maintain a sharp edge by sharpening the knife regularly, ideally every 1-2 weeks.
