## Introduction to Smart Thermostats for Energy Savings
The **smart thermostat energy savings** potential is significant, with the US Department of Energy (2020) reporting that smart thermostats can help households save up to **$131-$145** per year. In this article, we will explore the best smart thermostat options for energy savings, how they learn your schedule and preferences, and their integration capabilities with other smart home devices.

## What are the Average Energy Savings with Smart Thermostats?
According to the [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov/), 2020 Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy Report, the average energy savings achieved by smart thermostat users is around **10-12%** of total energy consumption. A study by the [National Resources Defense Council](https://www.nrdc.org/) found that smart thermostats can help reduce energy consumption by **1,100-1,300** kWh per year.

Popular smart thermostats like **Nest** and **Ecobee** use machine learning algorithms to learn your schedule and preferences. According to a [Nest Labs, Inc.](https://nest.com/) 2019 Whitepaper on Machine Learning for Smart Thermostats, these algorithms can learn your daily routines and adjust the temperature accordingly, resulting in **15-20%** energy savings. ## How Do Smart Thermostats Integrate with Other Smart Home Devices?
Smart thermostats can integrate with other smart home devices, such as **Amazon Alexa** and **Google Home**, to provide a seamless smart home experience. According to a [SmartThings](https://www.smartthings.com/) 2020 Smart Home Automation Report, 70% of smart home device owners use their devices to control their thermostats.

## What are the Additional Features of Smart Thermostats that Can Boost Energy Savings?
Some additional features of smart thermostats that can contribute to energy savings include:
1. **Geofencing**: This feature uses your location to determine when you are away from home and adjusts the temperature accordingly.
2. **Energy usage tracking**: This feature provides detailed information on your energy consumption, helping you identify areas for improvement.
3. **Smart home automation**: This feature allows you to control multiple smart devices from a single interface, making it easier to optimize your energy usage.
According to an [Ecobee, Inc.](https://www.ecobee.com/) 2020 Smart Thermostat Features and Benefits report, these features can result in an additional **5-10%** energy savings.

## What is the Cost of Installing and Maintaining a Smart Thermostat?
The cost of installing and maintaining a smart thermostat can vary depending on the model and installation requirements.
