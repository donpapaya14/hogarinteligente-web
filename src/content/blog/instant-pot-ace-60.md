## Sous Vide Egg Cooking with Instant Pot Ace 60
To cook perfect **sous vide eggs** using the Instant Pot Ace 60, follow these steps:
1. Fill the Instant Pot with water to the recommended level.
2. Set the temperature to 145°F (63°C) for large eggs.
3. Place the eggs in the Instant Pot and close the lid.
4. Cook for 45 minutes for large eggs.
As stated by [The Spruce Eats](https://www.thespruceeats.com), the key to cooking perfect sous vide eggs is to use the right temperature and cooking time.
