## Introduction to Dishwasher Energy Saving
The **dishwasher energy saving mistake** many of us make can lead to significant **dishwasher electricity waste**. According to the [Energy Star 2023 Dishwasher Efficiency Report](https://www.energystar.gov/), rinsing dishes before loading them into the dishwasher can waste up to **6,500 gallons of water** and cost around **$150/year** in energy. This is because modern dishwashers are designed to handle food residue without pre-rinsing.

## The Pre-Rinse Trap
Pre-rinsing dishes is a common practice, but it's unnecessary with modern dishwashers. The [Energy Star 2023 Dishwasher Efficiency Report](https://www.energystar.gov/) suggests that this habit can lead to significant water and energy waste. To avoid this, simply scrape off large food particles and load the dishes into the dishwasher.

## Sensor vs. Heat Dry
Using the 'heat dry' mode on your dishwasher can add up to **$30/year** to your energy bill compared to using the 'air-dry' or 'sensor-dry' alternatives. According to [Consumer Reports Dishwasher Tests 2024](https://www.consumerreports.org/), the performance difference between these modes is negligible. Here's a comparison:

| Heat Dry | $30/year |

## Load Pattern Matters
The way you load your dishwasher can also impact its efficiency. The **bowl-down placement mistake** can block water jets and force the dishwasher to run a second cycle. A study by the [University of Bonn Dishwasher Efficiency Study 2022](https://www.uni-bonn.de/) found that proper loading can reduce energy consumption by up to **15%**. 
1. Place bowls facing down to allow water to flow freely.
2. Arrange utensils in a separate container to prevent them from blocking water jets.

## Off-Peak Operation Hack
Delaying your dishwasher start time by **3 hours** can help you take advantage of utility off-peak rates, saving you up to **18%** per load without changing your habits. According to the [DOE Residential Energy Savings Database](https://www.energy.gov/), this simple hack can make a significant difference in your energy bill.

## Filter Neglect Penalty
A clogged filter can increase your dishwasher's runtime by **25%**. Cleaning the filter monthly can help maintain your dishwasher's efficiency. Here's how to do it in **90 seconds**:
1. Locate the filter at the bottom of the dishwasher.
2. Remove any large food particles.
3. Rinse the filter under running water.
