## Introduction to Pastry Brushes
Pastry brushes are essential tools for any baker, and choosing the right **pastry brush accessories** can make a significant difference in the quality of your baked goods. According to [King Arthur Flour](https://www.kingarthurflour.com), there are two main types of pastry brushes: natural and synthetic fibers. Natural fibers, such as boar or horse hair, are ideal for applying egg washes and melted butter, while synthetic fibers are better suited for applying water or milk-based mixtures.
