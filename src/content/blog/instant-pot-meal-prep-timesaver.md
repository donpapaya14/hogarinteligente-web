## Tips for Maximizing Instant Pot's Potential

From [The Kitchn (2022)](https://www.thekitchn.com):

1. **Layer ingredients strategically** - Dense foods (potatoes) at bottom, delicate ones (fish) on top
2. **Use minimum liquid** - Only 1 cup required for pressure cooking vs. 4+ cups for boiling
3. **Natural release for meats** - Prevents toughness (10-15 minutes for poultry, 15-20 for beef)
4. **Sauté first for flavor** - Browning onions/meats adds depth in 3-5 extra minutes

**Most Instant Pot meals prep in under 30 minutes**, including release time. For example, shredded chicken (15 minutes) and quinoa (12 minutes) can be cooked simultaneously using pot-in-pot method.

### What's the best size for meal prep?
**6-quart models suit 2-4 people**, according to Consumer Reports (2022). For larger families, the 8-quart [ Instant Pot Duo 8-Quart en Amazon](https://www.amazon.es/s?k=+Instant+Pot+Duo+8-Quart&tag=vladys-21) holds 50% more food for $30 extra.

### Can you cook frozen meat in Instant Pot?
**Yes, but add 50% more time**. Frozen chicken breasts take 18 minutes (vs. 12 minutes thawed) per USDA guidelines. Always check internal temperature reaches 165°F.

### How do you clean the Instant Pot?
**Remove the sealing ring and wash separately** to prevent odor transfer. The stainless steel pot is dishwasher safe, but wipe the base with damp cloth only.
