## Why Dishwasher Salt Matters
Dishwasher salt is crucial for maintaining your appliance's efficiency and lifespan. According to the [Consumer Reports Appliance Longevity Study (2022)](https://www.consumerreports.org/), using dishwasher salt can prevent limescale buildup, which damages heating elements and reduces efficiency. **Hard water** can cause significant damage, with **70%** of US households experiencing some level of water hardness.

## The #1 Mistake People Make
The most common mistake people make when using dishwasher salt is overfilling or underfilling the salt compartment. According to [Bosch Dishwasher Engineering Guidelines](https://www.bosch-home.com/), overfilling can cause corrosion, while underfilling leads to scale buildup. Both mistakes can cut your appliance's life by **2-3 years**.

## How to Check Your Salt Level Correctly
To check your salt level, follow these steps:
1. Locate the salt reservoir, usually at the bottom of the dishwasher.
2. Remove any visible debris or water from the reservoir.
3. Perform the 'spoon test': insert a spoon into the reservoir and scoop out some salt. If the salt is below the recommended level, refill it.

## The Right Type of Salt to Use
Only use **regenerating salt**, not table salt or rock salt. The ideal grain size is **1.5-2mm**, as recommended by the [European Water Softener Association Standards](https://www.ewsa.eu/).

## Money Saved by Fixing This
Proper salt use can save you **$150-$300** in repairs and extend your dishwasher's life by **4+ years**, resulting in a total savings of **$800+** on replacement, according to [HomeAdvisor Appliance Repair Cost Data (2023)](https://www.homeadvisor.com/).

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best dishwasher salt to use?
The best dishwasher salt to use is regenerating salt, with a grain size of 1.5-2mm. This type of salt is specifically designed for dishwashers and is effective in preventing limescale buildup.
### How often should I check my salt level?
You should check your salt level every **1-2 months**, depending on your usage. Regular checks can help prevent damage to your appliance.
### Can I use table salt in my dishwasher?
No, you should not use table salt in your dishwasher. Table salt is not designed for dishwashers and can cause damage to your appliance.
### What are the benefits of using dishwasher salt?
Using dishwasher salt can prevent limescale buildup, reduce damage to your appliance, and extend its lifespan. It can also improve the efficiency of your dishwasher.
### How much salt should I use in my dishwasher?
The amount of salt you should use in your dishwasher depends on the type of salt and the manufacturer's instructions. Generally, you should use **1-2 cups** of regenerating salt per month.
### Can I use rock salt in my dishwasher?
While rock salt can be used in dishwashers, it is not the most effective type of salt. Regenerating salt is specifically designed for dishwashers and is a better option.
