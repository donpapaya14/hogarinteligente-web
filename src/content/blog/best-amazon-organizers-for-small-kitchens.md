## Measuring Your Kitchen to Choose the Right Organizer
Measuring your kitchen is essential to choose the right organizer. As noted by [The Spruce](https://www.thespruce.com), it's crucial to measure the space between wall studs, which is typically 16 inches on center. To measure your kitchen, follow these steps:
1. Measure the width and length of your kitchen.
2. Measure the space between wall studs.
3. Measure the height of your ceilings.
