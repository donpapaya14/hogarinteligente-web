## Introduction to Color-Coded Home Filing System
A **color-coded home filing system** for quick retrieval is essential for maintaining a clutter-free and organized home office. According to a study by [Xerox Corporation](https://www.xerox.com), the brain processes color 60,000 times faster than text. This is why categorizing documents using colors like finances (red) and medical (blue) creates instant visual cues.

## Why Color-Coding Beats Alphabetical Filing
The key to an efficient filing system lies in its ability to provide quick access to documents. **Color-coding** achieves this by leveraging the brain's ability to recognize colors faster than text. For instance, a red folder can instantly signal financial documents, while a blue folder can indicate medical records.

## The 5 Essential Categories Every Home Needs
The [National Association of Productivity & Organizing Professionals](https://www.napo.net) recommends the following five essential categories for a home filing system:
1. **Taxes (yellow)**: includes tax returns, W-2 forms, and other tax-related documents
2. **Medical (blue)**: includes medical records, insurance documents, and prescriptions
3. **Home (green)**: includes documents related to home maintenance, appliance manuals, and warranties
4. **Financial (red)**: includes bank statements, investment documents, and credit card statements
5. **Personal (purple)**: includes identification documents, insurance policies, and other personal records

## How to Handle Overlapping Categories
When dealing with overlapping categories, follow the rule of filing under the **most used category**. For example, mortgage documents should be filed under **Financial (red)**, not **Home (green)**. Use cross-reference sticky notes to indicate the location of related documents.

## Quarterly Maintenance Ritual (15 Mins Max)
To maintain your color-coded home filing system, perform the following steps every quarter:
1. **Purge expired documents**: get rid of documents that are no longer needed or have expired
2. **Check label legibility**: ensure that all labels are clear and easy to read
3. **Consolidate orphaned papers**: organize any loose papers into their respective categories
Refer to the [IRS Publication 552 (2023)](https://www.irs.gov) for document retention periods.
