## Introduction to Pantry Organization
To reduce food waste by 50%, a well-planned **pantry organization system** is essential. This involves implementing the **First-In-First-Out (FIFO) method**, which ensures that older items are consumed before they expire. According to the [USDA FoodKeeper App](https://www.usda.gov/topics/food-safety), proper food storage can significantly reduce waste.

## The First-In-First-Out (FIFO) Method Explained
The FIFO method involves arranging food items in a way that the oldest items are at the front and are used first. This can be achieved by **labeling and rotating stock** regularly. Here are the practical steps to follow:
1. **Label each item** with the date it was purchased or opened.
2. **Rotate stock** every time you shop, moving the older items to the front.
3. **Check expiration dates** regularly and consume items before they expire.

## Clear Container Strategy for Visibility
Using **transparent bins** can reduce forgotten food by 30%, according to a study published in the [Journal of Consumer Psychology](https://www.journalofconsumerpsychology.com/). ## Expiration Date Tracking System
Creating a simple **color-coded sticker system** can help track expiration dates. Here's how to do it:
1. **Choose a color scheme**, such as red for soon-to-expire items and green for fresh items.
2. **Create stickers** with the chosen colors and apply them to the items.
3. **Check the stickers** regularly to ensure you consume items before they expire.
You can find a free printable template for this system on the [Harvard Food Law and Policy Clinic](https://www.chlpi.org/) website.

## Zone-Based Pantry Layout
Dividing your pantry into **zones** can help prevent overbuying and reduce waste. Here's how to do it:
1. **Daily use zone**: Store frequently used items, such as breakfast cereals and snacks.
2. **Bulk zone**: Store items in bulk, such as rice and pasta.
3. **Snack zone**: Store snacks, such as nuts and dried fruits.
According to the [National Association of Professional Organizers](https://www.napo.net/), this layout can help you save time and money.

## Monthly Pantry Audit Routine
Performing a monthly **pantry audit** can help identify soon-to-expire items and reduce waste. Here's a 15-minute checklist:
1. **Check expiration dates** and consume items before they expire.
2. **Identify soon-to-expire items** and plan meals around them.
3. **Repurpose ingredients** creatively, such as using leftover vegetables in a soup.
The [EPA Food Recovery Hierarchy](https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food) provides more information on reducing food waste.
