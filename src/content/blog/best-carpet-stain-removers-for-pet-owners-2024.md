According to the [American Cleaning Institute](https://www.cleaninginstitute.org), enzymes break down protein-based stains, while surfactants reduce surface tension, allowing for easier stain removal. A study by the University of Illinois (2018) found that **oxidizing agents** can remove up to **90%** of tough stains.

## DIY Carpet Stain Removal Methods
For a cost-effective and eco-friendly option, try these DIY methods:
* White vinegar and water solution: **1 cup white vinegar** to **1 cup water**
* Baking soda and water paste: **1 tablespoon baking soda** to **2 tablespoons water**
* Hydrogen peroxide solution: **1 part hydrogen peroxide** to **2 parts water**
According to [The Spruce](https://www.thespruce.com), these methods can remove up to **80%** of common carpet stains.

## Carpet Stain Removal Tips and Tricks
Here are some tips and tricks for removing carpet stains:
1. **Act quickly**: Remove stains within **24 hours** for best results.
2. **Blot, don't rub**: Use a clean cloth to blot the stain, rather than rubbing it in.
3. **Use the right cleaning solution**: Choose a solution that matches the type of stain and carpet fiber.
According to [Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com), these tips can increase the effectiveness of stain removal by up to **25%**.
