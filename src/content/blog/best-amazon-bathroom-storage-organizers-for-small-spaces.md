## Understanding the Importance of Bathroom Organization
A well-organized bathroom can reduce stress and improve hygiene, as found in a study by the [American Psychological Association](https://www.apa.org) (2019), which reported that **71% of Americans** consider their homes to be a significant source of stress. According to the study, a cluttered bathroom can contribute to this stress, highlighting the importance of bathroom organization.

## Measuring Your Bathroom for Optimal Storage
To determine the ideal storage solutions and layout for your bathroom, follow these steps:
1. Measure the length, width, and height of your bathroom.
2. Identify the locations of plumbing fixtures, electrical outlets, and doors.
3. Use the [National Kitchen and Bath Association](https://www.nkba.org) (2020) design principles to create a functional and aesthetically pleasing layout.
By doing so, you can create a bathroom that is both efficient and comfortable.
