## Introduction to Baking Soda
Baking soda, also known as sodium bicarbonate, is a **natural mineral** with a chemical composition of NaHCO3 and a pH level of **8.3**, making it a **mild base**. According to the [American Chemical Society](https://www.acs.org), baking soda works as a natural cleaner by neutralizing acidic substances and absorbing odors.

## Benefits of Baking Soda in Cleaning
The **antibacterial and antifungal properties** of baking soda make it an effective cleaner against a wide range of microorganisms. A study published in the [Journal of Environmental Health](https://www.jstor.org/journal/jenvheal) in 2018 found that baking soda can reduce the growth of **E. coli** and **Staphylococcus aureus** by **90%**. Additionally, baking soda is effective in removing **stains and odors** from surfaces.

## Making a Baking Soda Scrub
To make a baking soda scrub, mix **1 cup of baking soda** with **1/2 cup of water** to form a paste. You can customize the scrub by adding **essential oils** like lemon or tea tree oil for their antimicrobial properties. According to [Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com), you can also add **1 tablespoon of white vinegar** to enhance the cleaning power of the scrub.

## Using Baking Soda Scrub on Different Surfaces
Baking soda scrub can be used on various surfaces, including **countertops**, **sinks**, and **toilets**. However, it's essential to take precautions when using the scrub on **delicate surfaces** like marble or granite. The [Environmental Protection Agency](https://www.epa.gov) recommends testing the scrub on a small area before applying it to the entire surface.

## Combining Baking Soda with Other Natural Cleaners
Combining baking soda with other natural cleaners like **vinegar** and **essential oils** can enhance its effectiveness and create a holistic cleaning solution. According to the [National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences](https://www.niehs.nih.gov), mixing baking soda with vinegar can create a **chemical reaction** that helps to break down tough stains.

## Safety Precautions and Disposal
When using baking soda scrub, it's essential to take **safety precautions** to minimize environmental impact. The [Occupational Safety and Health Administration](https://www.osha.gov) recommends wearing **gloves** and **protective eyewear** when handling the scrub. Additionally, baking soda scrub should be disposed of in a **responsible manner**, such as by flushing it down the drain or disposing of it in a **recycling bin**.
