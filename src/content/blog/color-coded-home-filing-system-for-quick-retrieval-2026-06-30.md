## Introduction to Color-Coded Home Filing System
A **color-coded home filing system** can significantly improve the efficiency of document retrieval. Neuroscience research shows the brain processes colors **60% faster** than text, as demonstrated by the [Pantone Institute](https://www.pantone.com/) in 2021. This principle can be applied to home filing by assigning specific colors to different document types, such as **red for medical documents** and **blue for financial records**.

## Why Color-Coding Beats Alphabetical Filing
The brain's ability to process colors more quickly than text makes color-coding a more efficient method for filing. By using colors, individuals can quickly identify the category of a document without having to read the label. For example, a **color-coded system** might use:
- Red for medical documents
- Blue for financial records
- Green for household documents
- Yellow for personal documents
- Purple for tax records

## The 5 Essential Categories Everyone Needs
According to the [National Association of Professional Organizers](https://www.napo.net/) (NAA) 2023, there are five essential categories for a home filing system:
1. **Vital Documents**: passports, wills, and other important personal documents
2. **Tax Records**: tax returns, receipts, and other tax-related documents
3. **Medical Documents**: medical records, insurance claims, and prescriptions
4. **Household Documents**: appliance manuals, warranties, and other household-related documents
5. **Personal Documents**: school records, employment records, and other personal documents

## Where Most Systems Fail (and How to Fix It)
A common mistake in setting up a home filing system is using vague labels, such as 'Miscellaneous.' To avoid this, it is recommended to use the **'5-word max' rule** for labels, such as 'Car - Maintenance - Receipts 2024.' This helps to ensure that documents are filed in a specific and easily accessible location.

## Maintenance: The 10-Minute Monthly Refresh
To maintain a color-coded home filing system, it is recommended to set a monthly reminder to:
1. Purge expired documents, such as insurance EOBs after 1 year
2. Relocate misfiled items using the color guide
This process should take no more than **10 minutes** per month.
