## Smart Leak Detector for Washing Machines
### Why washing machines are a top source of leaks
According to the Insurance Information Institute 2023 study, water damage claims caused by washing machine failures account for 11% of all water damage claims, with an average repair cost of $3,400. This is due to the high water pressure and potential for hose rupture or pump failure.

### How smart leak detectors work under machines
Smart leak detectors use either probe or rope sensors to detect water leaks under washing machines. Probe sensors are inserted into the drain hose, while rope sensors are wrapped around the hose. Both types of sensors can be connected to a Wi-Fi system, sending alerts to your smartphone if a leak is detected.

### Critical features to compare
When selecting a smart leak detector, consider the following features:

| Feature | Description |

| Water Detection Sensitivity | The ability of the sensor to detect small leaks. |

| Automatic Shutoff Compatibility | The ability of the sensor to automatically shut off the water supply in the event of a leak. |

### Real-world performance test
A field test by SmartHomeLab in 2024 found that the average response time to detect a small leak was 30 seconds, while the average response time to detect a large leak was 10 seconds. However, the test also found that some sensors were prone to false alarms due to humidity or steam.

### Installation mistakes to avoid
When installing a smart leak detector, avoid the following mistakes:

1.  **Proper sensor placement**: Ensure that the sensor is placed in the correct location, either in the drain hose or wrapped around the hose.
2.  **Avoiding kinked hoses**: When routing cables, avoid kinking the hoses, which can cause false alarms or prevent the sensor from functioning properly.
3.  **Testing procedures**: Test the sensor regularly to ensure that it is functioning properly and sending accurate alerts.
