### minimalist under-sink organization with clear bins

**Why Clear Bins Beat Opaque Containers**

Research has shown that visual clutter can lead to increased stress levels and decreased productivity. In fact, a study published in the Journal of Environmental Psychology in 2020 found that people who lived in cluttered environments experienced higher levels of cortisol, a hormone associated with stress, compared to those who lived in organized environments. One of the main benefits of using clear bins is that they allow for quick access to the contents, reducing the time spent searching for what you need.

[World Health Organization](https://www.who.int) recommends that individuals aim to spend no more than 10 minutes per day searching for misplaced items. By using clear bins, you can reduce this time and increase your productivity.

### The 3-Zone Under-Sink System

To create an effective under-sink organization system, it's essential to divide the space into three zones: Cleaning, Maintenance, and Extras. This will help you to categorize your supplies and keep them organized.

### How to Store Tall Bottles Sideways

When storing tall bottles such as spray bottles, it's essential to store them sideways to prevent them from falling over. To do this, you can use a bin with dividers to separate the bottles and prevent them from touching each other.

**Measurements**: To prevent leaks and maximize space, it's essential to measure the bottles and the bin before storing them. A good rule of thumb is to store bottles that are 6 inches or less in height in a bin with a width of 12 inches.

### The 10-Item Rule for Under-Sink Minimalism

When it comes to decluttering your under-sink area, it's essential to follow the 10-item rule. This rule states that you should only keep 10 essential items in your under-sink area at any given time.

**Essential Categories**: The essential categories to include in your under-sink area are:

1. All-purpose cleaner
2. Glass cleaner
3. Disinfectant
4. Spare parts
5. Tools
6. Accessories
7. Backup sponge pack
8. Trash bags
9. Baking soda
10. Dish soap

**Ruthless Decluttering Criteria**: When decluttering your under-sink area, it's essential to follow the ruthless decluttering criteria. These criteria include:

* Expiry dates: Check the expiry dates of your cleaning supplies and dispose of any that are past their expiration date.
* Duplicates: Get rid of any duplicates of cleaning supplies.
* Unused items: Get rid of any items that you haven't used in the past year.
