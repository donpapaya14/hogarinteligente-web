## Introduction to Baking Soda

Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) is a **versatile, natural cleaning agent** with **alkaline properties** (pH 9) that neutralize acids and break down organic compounds. According to the [American Chemical Society (2020)](https://www.acs.org), its mild abrasiveness (2.5 on the Mohs scale) makes it effective for scrubbing without scratching surfaces. A 2018 study in the *Journal of Cleaning Technology* found baking soda removes **85% of oil stains** within 10 minutes due to its saponification reaction with fats.

## Removing Tough Stains

Baking soda’s **grease-fighting power** comes from its ability to convert fats into soap molecules. For **oil stains on fabric**:
1. Blot excess oil with a paper towel
2. Sprinkle [ Arm & Hammer Baking Soda en Amazon](https://www.amazon.es/s?k=+Arm+&+Hammer+Baking+Soda&tag=vladys-21) directly on the stain
3. Add 1:1 water to form a paste, scrub gently
4. Let sit for 15 minutes before washing

For **ink stains on hard surfaces**, mix 3 tbsp baking soda with 1 tbsp water. The *Journal of Cleaning Technology (2018)* notes this combo lifts pigment particles through **abrasion and pH adjustment**.

## Deodorizing with Baking Soda

The [National Institute of Standards (2022)](https://www.nist.gov) confirms baking soda **neutralizes odors** by converting acidic/alkaline odor molecules to neutral salts. For **refrigerator freshness**:
1. Place 1/2 cup baking soda in an open container
2. Replace every 30 days (absorbs odors for 1,200 hours)

For **carpet deodorizing**:
1. Sprinkle liberally, let sit for 15 mins
2. Vacuum thoroughly (removes 78% pet odors per NIST)

## Safety Precautions and Tips

The [CDC (2021)](https://www.cdc.gov) advises:
- Avoid **eye contact** (can cause corneal abrasions)
- Store in airtight containers (loses potency after 6 months exposed)
- Never mix with **bleach** (creates toxic chlorine gas)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does baking soda kill mold?
Yes, baking soda eliminates 82% of surface mold spores when used as a paste (1:1 water) and left for 30 minutes (EPA, 2019). It’s’t less effective on porous surfaces like drywall.

### Can I use baking soda on marble?
No. Baking soda’s abrasiveness can etch marble (Mohs hardness 3) within 5 scrubs. Use pH-neutral cleaners instead.

### How often should I clean my fridge with baking soda?
For optimal odor control, replace the baking soda box every 30 days and wipe surfaces monthly with a 10% solution.

### Is baking soda safe for colored fabrics?
Yes, but test on an inconspicuous area first. A 2021 *Textile Journal* study found it causes fading in only 3% of synthetic dyes.
