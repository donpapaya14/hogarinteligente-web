## Introduction to Solar Panel Cost and Savings Estimator
The **solar panel cost savings estimator** is a tool that helps homeowners and businesses determine the cost of installing solar panels and the potential energy savings. According to the [Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA)](https://www.seia.org), the average cost of solar panel installation in the US is around **$2.94 per watt**. 

## What are the Average Costs of Solar Panel Installation?
The average cost of solar panel installation in the US, including the cost per watt and the total cost of a typical solar panel system, is around **$18,000 to $20,000** for a 6-kilowatt system, according to the [Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA) 2022 report](https://www.seia.org).

## How Much Do Solar Panels Save on Energy Bills?
According to a [National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) 2020 study](https://www.nrel.gov), the average energy savings of solar panels is around **$400 to $1,000 per year**, depending on the location and system size. 

## What is the Realistic Payback Period for Solar Panels?
The average payback period of solar panels is around **6 to 10 years**, according to the [Database of State Incentives for Renewables and Efficiency (DSIRE) 2022 report](https://www.dsireusa.org). 

## How Do Solar Panel Efficiency and Panel Size Affect Savings?
According to the [International Energy Agency (IEA) 2019 report](https://www.iea.org), the efficiency of solar panels can affect energy savings, with **higher efficiency panels** producing more energy per unit area. 

## What Incentives and Tax Credits Are Available for Solar Panel Installation?
The federal government offers a **Solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC)** of up to **26%** of the total cost of the system, according to the [US Department of Energy 2022 website](https://www.energy.gov). 

## What Are the Maintenance and Repair Costs of Solar Panels?
According to the [Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA) 2022 report](https://www.seia.org), the average maintenance and repair costs of solar panels are around **$100 to $300 per year**.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How much do solar panels cost?
The cost of solar panels varies depending on the system size and location, but the average cost is around **$2.94 per watt**. 
### What are the benefits of solar panels?
Solar panels can save homeowners and businesses **$400 to $1,000 per year** on energy bills and reduce their carbon footprint. 
### How long do solar panels last?
Solar panels can last up to **25 years or more**, according to the [National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)](https://www.nrel.gov). 
### Can I install solar panels myself?
It is not recommended to install solar panels yourself, as it requires specialized knowledge and equipment, according to the [Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA)](https://www.seia.org). 
### What is the best solar panel system for my home?
The best solar panel system for your home depends on your energy needs and budget, but a **6 kW system** is a good starting point for most homes. 
### How do I maintain my solar panels?
You can maintain your solar panels by cleaning them regularly and inspecting them for damage, according to the [International Energy Agency (IEA)](https://www.iea.org).
