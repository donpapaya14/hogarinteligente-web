## Understanding Pet Hair and Allergies
Pet hair and dander are common allergens that can significantly affect indoor air quality. According to the American Academy of Allergy, Asthma, and Immunology (AAAAI), **60% of households in the United States have at least one pet**. This can lead to increased allergy symptoms such as sneezing, congestion, and itchy eyes.

The science behind pet hair and allergies lies in the proteins found in the dander, saliva, and urine of pets. These proteins can become airborne and be inhaled, triggering allergic reactions. The AAAAI recommends regular cleaning and vacuuming to reduce pet dander and hair.

## Tips for Maintaining Your Robot Vacuum
To keep your robot vacuum running efficiently, follow these practical tips:
1. **Clean the brushes and filters regularly** to prevent clogging and maintain suction power.
2. **Update the software** to ensure you have the latest features and improvements.
3. **Check for blockages** in the vacuum's path to prevent damage and maintain cleaning efficiency.

## Pet Hair Cleaning Hacks
In addition to using a robot vacuum, here are some extra tips for cleaning pet hair on hardwood floors:
1. **Use a traditional vacuum** with a pet hair attachment to deep clean high-traffic areas.
2. **Use a damp mop** to pick up loose pet hair and dander.
3. **Use a pet hair-specific cleaning product** to reduce shedding and dander.
