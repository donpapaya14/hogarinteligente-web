## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I store avocados to keep them fresh?
To store avocados and keep them fresh, use the Chefn FreshForce Avocado Saver, which can reduce oxygen exposure by up to **90%**. You can also store them in the refrigerator to slow down the ripening process.
### Can I use the Chefn FreshForce Avocado Saver for other fruits and vegetables?
Yes, the Chefn FreshForce Avocado Saver can be used to store other fruits and vegetables, such as lemons, onions, and guacamole. It can keep them fresh for a longer period by preventing oxygen from reaching them.
### How long do avocados last in the refrigerator?
Avocados can last for **5-7 days** in the refrigerator, depending on their ripeness and storage conditions. You can check their freshness by gently squeezing them; if they are slightly soft to the touch, they are ripe and ready to eat.
### Can I freeze avocados?
Yes, you can freeze avocados, but they will not retain their creamy texture. Frozen avocados are best used in smoothies or as a topping for dishes. To freeze avocados, slice or mash them and store them in an airtight container or freezer bag.
### How do I ripen avocados quickly?
To ripen avocados quickly, store them in a paper bag with an apple or banana. The ethylene gas produced by these fruits will help ripen the avocados within **2-3 days**.
### What is the best way to store guacamole?
To store guacamole, use an airtight container and press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole to prevent air from reaching it. You can also add a squeeze of lemon juice to prevent browning.
