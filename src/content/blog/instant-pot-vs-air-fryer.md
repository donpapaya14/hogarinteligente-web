## Introduction to Instant Pot and Air Fryer
When it comes to **kitchen gadget comparison**, two devices that often come up in conversation are the Instant Pot and the Air Fryer. The **Instant Pot** is a pressure cooker that can also be used for sautéing, steaming, and slow cooking, while the **Air Fryer** is a device that uses little to no oil to fry foods. According to [Amazon Product Descriptions 2024](https://www.amazon.com), both devices have their own set of benefits and functions.
