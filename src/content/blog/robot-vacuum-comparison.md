## Introduction to Robot Vacuums

Robot vacuums have revolutionized home cleaning, offering **hands-free convenience** and **efficient floor maintenance**. Key features to consider include **battery life** (60-120 minutes), **navigation technology** (LIDAR vs. camera-based), and **dustbin capacity** (0.4-0.8L). For budget-conscious buyers, the **best cheap robot vacuums** balance performance and reliability under $300.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best robot vacuum under $200?
The **Eufy RoboVac 11** ($159) is the top pick under $200, with **100-minute runtime** and **BoostIQ suction** ([Amazon Best Sellers 2024](https://www.amazon.com)). Avoid models without brush rolls for carpet cleaning.

### Do cheap robot vacuums work on carpets?
Yes, but performance varies. The **Roomba 675** removes **94% of pet hair** from medium-pile carpets, while budget mopping robots like the **ILIFE V3s Pro** struggle ([Pet Hair Guide 2022](https://www.pethairguide.com)).

### How often should I clean my robot vacuum?
Empty the dustbin **every 2-3 cleaning cycles** and clean brushes **weekly** to maintain suction. Replace filters every **3 months** ([iFixit 2023](https://www.ifixit.com)).

### Can robot vacuums replace traditional vacuums?
For daily maintenance, yes. However, **deep cleaning** still requires traditional vacuums 1-2 times monthly, especially for high-pile carpets ([Consumer Reports 2023](https://www.consumerreports.org)).

### Are robot vacuums safe for hardwood floors?
Yes. Models like the **Eufy RoboVac 11** have **soft rubber brushes** that won’t scratch surfaces. Avoid units with stiff bristle brushes ([Wirecutter 2023](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter)).
